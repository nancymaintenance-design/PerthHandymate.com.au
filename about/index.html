About Ellis Services Group

About Ellis Services Group

A practical Perth point of contact for property maintenance, repairs and improvement requests that need a clear path forward.

One accountable starting point

Property work is easier to progress when the next step is clear

Ellis Services Group helps people make sense of property work before it starts. We listen to what needs attention, clarify the property and scope, then help establish the appropriate delivery pathway for the request.

For many customers, the value is not in guessing which trade to call first. It is having one team that can start the conversation, ask practical questions and keep the next step visible.

Based in Perth

We support requests across the Perth metropolitan area. Serviceability, availability and the right delivery pathway are confirmed for each individual job.

How we work

Clear communication before work proceeds

Start with the real issue

Customers can describe the outcome they need, what they have observed and any access considerations. A precise trade label is not required to begin the conversation.

Make the scope visible

We encourage a clear brief, including location, photos where safe, approximate measurements and timing constraints, so the next decision is based on useful information.

Confirm the pathway

Work may be delivered by Ellis teams or reviewed service partners. The attending provider and any scope-specific requirements are confirmed before work proceeds.

From first contact to the next step

How a request moves forward

01

Tell us what needs attention

Share the task, property location and the outcome you are trying to achieve.

02

Clarify the property and scope

We may ask about condition, access, quantities, photos or other practical details.

03

Identify the suitable pathway

We review the request against local coverage, the work involved and available delivery options.

04

Confirm before work proceeds

The provider, scope and any relevant next steps are made clear before the booking moves ahead.
