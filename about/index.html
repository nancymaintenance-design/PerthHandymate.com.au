About Ellis Services Group

The Perth office of Ellis Services Group, with a local handyman and maintenance team for direct property repairs and maintenance.

Ellis Group · Perth office

Direct local handyman and maintenance services

Ellis Perth is part of Ellis Services Group and operates as an independent Perth office with its own local handyman and maintenance team. We take service requests directly and discuss the job, property and scope with you.

We are not a lead-generation, brokerage or referral platform. You deal directly with our Perth office and local team from the first conversation through to the agreed work.

Based in Perth

We support property maintenance, repairs and improvement requests across the Perth metropolitan area.

How we work

How Our Perth Local Team Handles a Job

Start with the real issue

Customers can describe the outcome they need, what they have observed and any access considerations. A precise trade label is not required to begin the conversation.

Make the scope visible

We encourage a clear brief, including location, photos where safe, approximate measurements and timing constraints, so the next decision is based on useful information.

Confirm the work

Our local team confirms the practical scope, access and next step directly with you.
