Window Repairs in Perth: Common Questions

Repairs and work we can assess

  • Adjust or repair hinges, stays, rollers, locks and balances
  • Replace suitable seals, catches or other worn window hardware
  • Repair localised frame or sill damage after identifying moisture sources
Can you repair a timber window that sticks or will not open?

We check the frame, sash and hardware to identify where movement is restricted. The repair depends on whether the issue is alignment, worn fittings or damaged timber. Tell us which window is affected and whether it sticks when opening, closing or latching.

Can a damaged window frame be repaired instead of replaced?

We assess the damaged section and surrounding timber before recommending a local repair or replacement. The amount of sound material remaining and the way the window operates matter. We explain what work is needed before you approve the written quote.

Do you repair window locks, handles and hinges?

We check the existing fittings and confirm compatible replacements or adjustments. The window type and hardware condition determine the approach. Let us know whether the window is difficult to secure, the handle is loose or a hinge is preventing normal movement.

Is broken glass the same as a window-frame repair?

No. Glass replacement differs from timber, alignment or hardware repairs. Keep clear of broken or loose glass and tell Ellis where it is fitted. We assess the panel, frame and safe access before confirming the appropriate work. Security hardware and major frame damage also need their own scope checked.

Can you assess several windows before a rental inspection or sale?

Yes. Tell us which windows stick, have damaged fittings or need timber repairs, and we can assess the list together. For a rental property, identify who can approve the work and arrange access. We confirm the repair scope and quote before proceeding.

What determines the price, and do I need to send photos?

Window size, frame condition, hardware, access and the number of repairs affect the quote. We inspect the windows on site. Photos are optional; a short description and your suburb are enough to start the conversation.
