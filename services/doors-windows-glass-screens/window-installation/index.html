Window Installation — Work & Quote Details

Replacing an old window within the existing opening

Ellis measures the opening and checks the frame, surrounding finish and access. We discuss the replacement product and how it will operate. Removing the old unit, fitting and making good are identified in the installation scope.

Repair the frame or replace the whole window?

A sticking sash or worn fitting may be a repair job rather than a complete replacement. Rot, widespread damage or a different product design may change the recommendation. Glass, hardware and frame work are assessed separately.

Window Installation service guide

Window Installation in Perth: Common Questions

Can an old window be replaced within the existing opening?

A replacement can be assessed against the opening dimensions, wall construction and surrounding condition. We confirm the frame, glazing and opening style before agreeing removal and installation. Changes to the structural opening require separate assessment.

Should a sticking timber window be repaired or replaced?

The frame, hardware and extent of damage need checking before that decision. A serviceable window may suit repair; extensive deterioration may make replacement more appropriate. We assess the existing condition and confirm the proposed scope.

What details matter for an upstairs window replacement?

The storey level, access and wall construction affect the installation plan. Glazing and elevated work require qualified people and suitable controls. Energy, bushfire or heritage requirements may also affect product selection and must be checked where applicable.
