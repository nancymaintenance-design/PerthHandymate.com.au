Skylights in Perth: Common Questions

Can a skylight be added to a dark room?

A suitable location depends on the roof type, ceiling layout and the light or ventilation you want. We assess the room and roof before confirming the unit and installation scope. Roof access and weatherproofing requirements form part of that assessment.

Does a leaking skylight always need replacing?

Not always. Flashing, seals and surrounding junctions need checking, along with possible condensation. Tell us when moisture appears and the existing unit details if known. The assessment determines whether a component repair or replacement is appropriate.

Do powered skylight blinds need an electrician?

Fixed electrical work or nearby wiring requires an appropriately licensed electrician. Tell us if the existing or proposed unit has powered blinds or controls. The electrical requirements must be confirmed alongside roof and ceiling work.
