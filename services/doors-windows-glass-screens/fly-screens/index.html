Fly Screens & Repairs in Perth: Common Questions

Repairs and work we can assess

  • Remesh torn or brittle insect screens
  • Repair or replace damaged frames, corners, rollers and handles
  • Measure and fit screens for compatible windows or doors
Can you replace torn mesh without replacing the frame?

Yes, where the existing frame and fittings are suitable for reuse. We check the frame, corners and mesh-retaining spline before recommending remeshing or a replacement screen. This keeps the repair focused on the damaged parts.

What can you do about a flyscreen door that drags or will not close?

We check the screen-door rollers, track, frame and handles to identify what is stopping it moving or closing properly. Adjustment, replacement hardware or frame repair depends on the condition we find. A heavy glass sliding door is a different job: tell us whether the problem affects the insect screen or the glass door.

Can you fit screens to windows as well as doors?

We measure the opening and check how the screen attaches before confirming a suitable fit. Fixed window screens, hinged doors and sliding screen panels have different fittings. Tell us which openings need attention and whether you want to repair existing screens or fit new ones.

Which mesh should I choose for pets or a coastal home?

Tell us about pets, exposure and your preferred mesh at the assessment. We check the screen design and compatible material options before quoting. An insect screen should not be treated as a security screen; product-specific security requirements need a separate check.

What affects the cost of flyscreen repairs?

The number and size of screens, mesh choice, reusable frames, replacement hardware and access all affect the work. We assess the screens on site and provide a written quote. You can ask us to assess several damaged screens together, including before a rental inspection.

Do I need measurements or photos before contacting Ellis?

No. Tell us which screen is damaged and your suburb, and we can arrange an on-site assessment. Existing photos or approximate measurements are optional. You do not need to remove a screen or reach a difficult opening to prepare a request.

Flyscreen Mesh Replacement & Screen Door Repairs in Perth

Torn mesh, loose spline or a damaged frame

Replacing flyscreen mesh and repairing a bent frame are different jobs. Tell Ellis which windows or doors are affected and whether the screen still fits its opening. We check the mesh, frame and ordinary screen hardware before agreeing the repair.

Dragging screen doors versus glass sliding doors

Identify whether the light insect screen or heavy glass panel is dragging. Ordinary flyscreen repairs do not establish that a security screen or glass sliding door can use the same repair method. Ellis confirms the correct work and assessment with you.
