Fly Screens & Repairs

Fly-screen services cover new mesh screens, remeshing, frame repairs and adjustments to fixed, sliding or hinged screen units.

  • Remesh torn or brittle insect screens
  • Repair or replace damaged frames, corners, rollers and handles
  • Measure and fit screens for compatible windows or doors

Shower Screens

Shower-screen work includes measuring, installation, adjustment and replacement of framed, semi-frameless or frameless enclosures.

  • Measure the shower opening and check wall and floor conditions
  • Install a suitable screen, door and hardware system
  • Adjust hinges, rollers, seals and alignment on serviceable screens

Door Installation

Door-installation services cover internal and external doors, frames, hinges, hardware and adjustments needed for a secure, smooth fit.

  • Measure the opening and check frame condition and clearances
  • Fit a compatible hinged, sliding or cavity door system
  • Install or adjust hinges, handles, locks, closers and weather seals

Skylights

Skylight services can cover new units, replacements, leaking junctions and ventilation or light improvements in suitable roof locations.

  • Assess roof type, ceiling layout and proposed skylight position
  • Install or replace compatible skylight and flashing components
  • Investigate leaks, condensation or failed seals around an existing unit

Window Installation

Window installation covers measuring and fitting replacement or new window units while addressing openings, flashings, seals and finishes.

  • Measure the structural opening and select a compatible window configuration
  • Remove and replace an existing unit within an agreed scope
  • Install flashings, seals and trims appropriate to the wall system

Window Repairs

Window repair addresses sticking sashes, failed rollers, damaged hardware, draughts, seals and localised frame defects where the unit remains serviceable.

  • Adjust or repair hinges, stays, rollers, locks and balances
  • Replace suitable seals, catches or other worn window hardware
  • Repair localised frame or sill damage after identifying moisture sources

Door Repairs or New Door Installation?

A rubbing timber door, loose hinge or worn handle is different from supplying and fitting a new door. Explore minor door and hardware repairs or new door installation.

If a sliding door drags, tell Ellis whether the problem is the insect screen or the heavy glass panel. Flyscreen repair covers ordinary screens and mesh. For a glass panel, describe the rollers, track or closing problem so we can confirm the appropriate assessment and repair arrangements.
