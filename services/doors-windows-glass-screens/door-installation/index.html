Door Installation in Perth: Common Questions

Can a new internal door be fitted to the existing frame?

It may be possible if the frame is sound and the opening suits the new door. We measure the opening and check hinges, clearances and the swing area. Damaged or distorted frames may require additional work within the agreed scope.

Do I need a replacement door if the bedroom door rubs after new carpet?

Not necessarily. The door, hinges, frame and new floor clearance need assessment first. An adjustment may be enough, depending on the door construction and condition. We confirm whether adjustment or replacement is suitable before agreeing the work.

Are entry, security or fire-rated doors fitted in the same way as internal doors?

These doors can have specific product, hardware, fixing and compliance requirements. Tell us the door location and any known rating or security requirement. Relevant qualifications and any required licences must be checked; regulated lock work is not assumed to be ordinary door fitting.
