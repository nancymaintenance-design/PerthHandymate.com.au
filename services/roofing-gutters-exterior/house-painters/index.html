Assessment details for interior & exterior house painting

Touch-ups, colour matching and painting scope

Small painting jobs can cover scuffed walls, door painting, trims and areas repaired after damage. Surface preparation, existing paint and the required finish determine whether a local touch-up is suitable or a wider area needs repainting.

Ellis assesses paint work as its own scope, including relevant registration requirements. Those requirements depend on the applicable jurisdiction and contract value; see WA painting registration guidance. A handyman label does not decide whether a painting contract can be accepted.

Peeling paint after adhesive hooks and a rental patch that needs matching paint both need the remaining coating assessed. Share any retained paint details if available. Colour matching can help select a coating but cannot promise that aged surfaces will blend invisibly.

Why a touch-up differs

Colour, sheen, ageing, texture and lighting can make new paint look different from the existing wall. Preparation and application also affect the result. Ellis discusses the visible repair area and whether a larger repaint is preferable before agreeing the finish.

Check painting requirements for the actual job

WA painting registration depends on the work, contract value and applicable area. Check WA painting registration guidance against the proposed project. Ellis confirms the appropriate personnel and scope before acceptance; splitting a job is not a substitute for checking its requirements.

House Painters service guide

House Painters: Common Questions

Can a small paint repair match the rest of the wall?

A touch-up can remain visible because colour, sheen, aged coatings and light affect the result. Ellis checks the existing finish before you choose a local repair or a wider repaint. See planning the paint finish after wall repairs; an exact match is not assumed.

Can walls, ceilings, doors and trims be included in one painting enquiry?

List the rooms and surfaces you want painted, along with your colour and finish preferences. Ellis assesses surface preparation and coating compatibility before confirming the agreed areas and quote.

What happens if exterior paint is peeling or the surface is damp?

The surface and moisture concerns need assessment before preparation or coating. Older paint may contain lead, and work at height needs suitable controls. Tell Ellis the condition and any previous coating or moisture problems.

Why does touch-up paint look different from the wall?

Ageing, sheen, texture, light and preparation can make a touch-up visible even with a similar colour. Ellis assesses the existing coating and discusses local repair or wider repainting. An exact match is not assumed.

How are WA painting registration requirements checked?

Check the proposed work, contract value and applicable area against WA painting registration guidance. Ellis confirms the relevant requirements for the actual scope before acceptance. A small-job description alone does not establish an exemption or a company registration number.
