Interior & Exterior House Painting — Work & Quote Details

Touch-ups after repairs or a room repaint

Tell Ellis whether you want patched walls, door trim or an entire room painted. We assess the existing coating and required finish. A touch-up on aged paint may remain visible, so the repair area and blending expectations are agreed first.

Peeling exterior paint or damp surfaces

Preparation depends on the condition, not only the colour choice. Loose coatings and recurring moisture need assessment before repainting. We identify cleaning, filling, sanding and any prior repair work in the scope, together with the paint system.

House Painters in Perth: Common Questions

Can a small paint repair match the rest of the wall?

The existing colour, sheen, coating condition and light can affect the appearance of a touch-up. Tell Ellis which area needs painting and any paint details you have. The assessment confirms whether a local repair or wider repaint is suitable.

Can walls, ceilings, doors and trims be included in one painting enquiry?

List the rooms and surfaces you want painted, along with your colour and finish preferences. Ellis assesses surface preparation and coating compatibility before confirming the agreed areas and quote.

What happens if exterior paint is peeling or the surface is damp?

The surface and moisture concerns need assessment before preparation or coating. Older paint may contain lead, and work at height needs suitable controls. Tell Ellis the condition and any previous coating or moisture problems.
