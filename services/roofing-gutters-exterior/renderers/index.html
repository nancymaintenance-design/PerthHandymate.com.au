Renderers service guide

Renderers in Perth: Common Questions

Can a small damaged area of render be repaired?

A localised repair may be suitable if the underlying masonry and surrounding render are sound. Tell Ellis the wall location, finish type and damage. The assessment confirms the repair area and suitable render system.

Why should damp or recurring render cracks be assessed first?

Damp, persistent cracking or hollow areas can indicate a moisture or substrate problem. Covering the surface alone may leave the cause unresolved. Tell Ellis about previous repairs and any changes in the cracks before resurfacing is considered.

Can repaired render match the existing wall texture?

The existing texture and finish need inspection before a matching approach can be agreed. Share the finish type, colour and age if known. The assessment confirms the wall area and expected finish; an exact match should not be assumed.
