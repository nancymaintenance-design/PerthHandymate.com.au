Pavers service guide

Pavers in Perth: Common Questions

Can sunken or lifted pavers be repaired without replacing the whole area?

Localised lifting and relaying may be suitable after the cause is assessed. Tell Ellis where the paving is uneven and any known root, drainage or settlement issues. The assessment confirms the base repairs and paving scope.

What should I share when planning a new paved path or outdoor area?

Tell Ellis how the area will be used, your paver preference and any material-access limits. Existing measurements are helpful but optional. Site levels, drainage and the base need assessment before the layout and quote are confirmed.

Why does uneven paving need more than a surface repair?

Ground movement, roots or poor drainage can affect the base beneath the pavers. Assessing the cause helps define suitable preparation. Service locations also need checking before excavation.
