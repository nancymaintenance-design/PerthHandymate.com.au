Cladding Repairs & Replacement

Cladding work can address damaged exterior boards or panels, localised weather exposure, replacement sections and new wall-finish enquiries.

  • Replace compatible cladding boards or panels within an agreed scope
  • Detail corners, openings and flashings as part of an exterior envelope job

Exterior

Gutter Cleaning, Repairs & Replacement

Ellis cleans, repairs and replaces blocked, leaking, rusted or loose gutters and downpipes in Perth. We check the cause of overflow or poor drainage and confirm which sections need cleaning, resealing or replacement.

  • Remove accessible leaf build-up and check outlets for blockages
  • Repair leaking joins, loose brackets and localised damaged sections
  • Replace deteriorated gutters or downpipes and review falls and discharge points

Interior & Exterior House Painting

House painting can cover interior rooms, exterior surfaces, trims and repainting programs after preparation and coating compatibility are assessed.

  • Prepare surfaces by cleaning, filling and sanding where suitable
  • Apply compatible primers and finish coats to agreed areas
  • Paint walls, ceilings, trims or exterior elements to the selected scheme

Exterior

Paving Installation & Repairs

Paving services cover new paths and outdoor areas, lifted or sunken sections, edging and repairs where the base and drainage are suitable.

  • Prepare or repair the base for a stable paved surface

  • Lift and relay localised uneven sections after investigating the cause

Roofing & Roof Repairs

Roofing enquiries include leak investigation, damaged tiles or sheets, flashings, roof penetrations and larger repair or replacement scopes.

  • Inspect accessible roof coverings, ridges, flashings and penetrations
  • Repair or replace damaged tiles, sheets or localised components
  • Trace likely water-entry paths and address agreed roof defects

Build & Renovate

Wall Rendering & Render Repairs

Rendering services apply or repair compatible exterior and interior masonry finishes after the substrate and moisture conditions are assessed.

  • Prepare masonry and apply a selected render system
  • Repair localised hollow, cracked or damaged render where suitable
  • Match texture and finish across an agreed wall area
