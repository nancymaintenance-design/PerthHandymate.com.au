Cladding service guide

Cladding in Perth: Common Questions

Can damaged cladding boards or panels be replaced individually?

Localised replacement may be suitable if compatible material is available and the surrounding wall is sound. Tell Ellis which wall is affected and the material type if known. The assessment confirms the repair area and finish.

What should be checked when cladding leaks near a window or roof?

The cladding, openings and nearby flashings need assessment together. Tell Ellis where moisture appears and when it occurs so the inspection can consider the junctions before a repair scope is agreed.

Should old cladding be disturbed before an assessment?

Leave suspect material undisturbed. Older cladding may contain hazardous materials, and work at height needs safe access. Share the building age and material details you already know with Ellis; safely taken existing photos are optional.
