What we can coordinate

A practical route for surveyors

Surveying services provide measured site and boundary information for property, design and construction decisions.

Common requests

  • Undertake a feature and level survey for design inputs
  • Locate or re-establish boundaries where the requested survey permits
  • Set out approved construction points or prepare relevant survey documentation

Safety and qualifications

Boundary and cadastral work must be completed by appropriately registered or licensed surveyors where required; site hazards must be disclosed before attendance.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
