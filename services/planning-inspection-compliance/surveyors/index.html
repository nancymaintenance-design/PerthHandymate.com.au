Surveyors in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Surveyors?

Surveying services provide measured site and boundary information for property, design and construction decisions.

  • Undertake a feature and level survey for design inputs
  • Locate or re-establish boundaries where the requested survey permits
  • Set out approved construction points or prepare relevant survey documentation
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Property address, title details and purpose of the survey
  • Plans, previous surveys or design documents available
  • Required deliverable, project stage and site-access constraints
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Boundary and cadastral work must be completed by appropriately registered or licensed surveyors where required; site hazards must be disclosed before attendance.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
