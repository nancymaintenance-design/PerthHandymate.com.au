Building Inspectors in Perth: Common Questions

Can an inspection focus on a particular defect?

A targeted inspection can be scoped around a stated condition concern. Describe the location, symptoms and any changes so the accessible areas and reporting brief can be agreed.

Does a building inspection cover hidden defects and every specialist system?

The inspection covers the agreed scope and accessible conditions. Pest, structural, electrical or hazardous-material concerns may need separate specialist assessment; concealed conditions cannot be assumed to have been inspected.
