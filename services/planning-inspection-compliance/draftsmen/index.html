Draftsmen in Perth: Common Questions

Do I need concept drawings or construction drawings?

The drawing purpose determines the package. Explain whether you are exploring a layout, seeking approval or preparing for construction, and include any council or consultant feedback.

Do drafting drawings replace engineering and survey documents?

Those are separate professional inputs where the project requires them. Drafting can incorporate consultant information, but engineering, surveying and statutory certification need the appropriate practitioners.
