Building Inspectors

Building inspections provide an independent visual assessment of accessible property conditions for purchase, maintenance or construction-stage decisions.

  • Inspect accessible interior, exterior, roof-space or subfloor areas as scoped
  • Document visible defects, maintenance concerns and limitations
  • Complete a pre-purchase, handover or targeted condition inspection

Draftsmen

Drafting services turn a renovation, addition or new-build brief into measured drawings and documentation suitable for design coordination or approvals.

  • Measure and document existing property conditions
  • Prepare concept, planning or construction drawings to the agreed stage
  • Coordinate drawing updates from consultant and client information

Structural Engineers

Structural-engineering services provide analysis and design for building elements, movement, alterations and load-bearing concerns.

  • Assess cracking, movement or a proposed structural alteration
  • Design beams, footings, walls or other structural elements for a defined project
  • Prepare calculations, drawings or inspection advice within the agreed brief
