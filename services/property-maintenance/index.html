Property Maintenance in Perth

Ellis Perth helps homeowners, landlords and property managers organise agreed repair lists through one service contact. We assess the property, confirm who approves the work and provide a written quote for the maintenance tasks you need.

Discuss property maintenance

Home Maintenance: Household Repair Lists

Group the jobs around your home by room and priority: a sticking door, loose cupboard hardware, wall damage or an outdoor fitting. Describe the problem rather than trying to name the trade. We check which tasks can be combined and which need separate specialist assessment.

If you are arranging work for a family member, identify who will be present, who makes the decisions and who receives the quote. Our small repair service explains common tasks; the service directory covers larger or specialist scopes.

Rental Property Maintenance & Tenant Access

Landlords and property managers can provide an approved maintenance list, inspection notes or a description of each fault. Identify the authorised contact, any purchase-order requirement and which items need a separate quote. A tenant reporting a fault or opening the property does not automatically approve extra repairs.

Before the visit

Agree the tenant appointment, key collection and return, parking and entry instructions with Ellis. Keep access arrangements with the people responsible for the property. Photos can help explain a fault but are optional; our team can inspect on site.

Vacate and handover repairs

Separate repair tasks from cleaning requirements and identify the handover date. We confirm the accepted list and timing directly. Maintenance repairs do not guarantee a bond outcome; any additional damage found needs a revised scope and approval.

Strata Maintenance: Shared-property Approval

For strata maintenance, state whether the defect is inside a lot or affects common property, and identify the person authorised to approve the particular work. Shared doors, entry points and external fixtures may involve different responsibilities. Ellis confirms the permission and access arrangements before proceeding; a resident's request alone does not establish common-property approval.

List any building access restrictions, working-hour arrangements and protection required around occupied areas. Where the approval or repair responsibility is unclear, resolve it with the authorised property contact before the job is booked.

Agree the work, materials and completion details

After assessment we identify the repairs, proposed materials, access and any specialist requirements in the written quote. Regulated work is completed by Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel within the agreed scope. Extra repairs or changed materials are discussed for approval before proceeding.

Tell us what completion information you need, such as a summary against the approved list or photos where agreed. If you want repeat maintenance, discuss the tasks and preferred interval; we confirm the scope and scheduling directly. No ongoing attendance arrangement is assumed from a single enquiry.

Assessment details for property maintenance

Plan maintenance around the property condition

Property maintenance combines agreed routine repairs and preventative checks: door operation, fittings, exposed timber, visible leaks and seasonal outdoor condition. It is a defined task list rather than an inspection guarantee or an unlimited maintenance package.

For an owner overseas or a requested monthly arrangement, nominate the approval contact, access arrangements, reporting needs and preferred interval. Ellis confirms scheduling and scope directly; ask for completion photos where required rather than assume a reporting package.

A rental with recurring loose hardware and weathered outdoor fittings benefits from a record of earlier repairs. Separate a repeat symptom from a new fault so the assessment can consider the cause as well as the immediate repair.

What the maintenance list includes

A maintenance list can include suitable minor repairs, fittings and checks of exposed or frequently used parts. The quote identifies each accepted task, specialist work, access and completion information; it does not automatically cover every building system.

Choosing a review interval

Inspection frequency depends on use, exposure, material and previous faults. Review changes after weather events or a reported problem, and discuss a suitable repeat interval. A loose or unsafe item should be assessed before the next routine visit.

Rental lists, remote owners and completion information

Rental property maintenance starts with the authorised repair list and approval contact. Door, cupboard and wall repairs can be assessed together, but each property needs its own access, permission and work record.

Property managers can approve the written scope before work starts and nominate how variations are approved. For overseas owners, agree the contact and completion information directly. Photos and summaries are available only as agreed, not an assumed reporting package.

An inspection list covering several rental properties needs each address and fault kept distinct. Tenant appointments, key handling and any purchase-order requirement are agreed privately with the responsible contact; an occupant providing entry does not approve extra work.

Useful completion photos

Where agreed, a completion record should identify the repair item and location, show the relevant before and after condition and note incomplete or separately approved work. Avoid including occupants, keys or private documents. Photos do not replace the written scope or prove concealed work.

Approval before attendance and work

Nominate the landlord, property manager or other person authorised for the particular repair and quote. Access arrangements and spending approval are separate. Ellis confirms who can approve the work and variations before proceeding with the agreed repair list.

Vacate repairs follow the approved list

End-of-lease and handover repairs can include approved nail-hole patches, damaged cupboard fittings, flyscreen mesh and sticking doors. Cleaning and repairs have different checklists and should be identified separately.

Use the property manager’s marked list where available and confirm the person approving the quote. Ellis checks the desired finish, materials and deadline before accepting the scope. Repair timing and any changes are confirmed directly rather than inferred from an inspection date.

Before a final inspection, list wall holes, torn mesh and damaged cupboard doors by room. State what has been approved and the handover date. A last-minute repair request needs the appointment and accepted work confirmed directly; the repair does not determine the inspection result.

Agree repairs before changes

A tenant should confirm the approved repair scope with the authorised property contact before altering fittings or finishes. Permission to enter is not permission to change the property. Written approval helps separate accepted work from disputed responsibility or additional requests.

Repair and bond decisions

A repair addresses the agreed physical work; a bond decision can involve condition records, cleaning, responsibility and other matters outside that repair. Ellis does not guarantee a bond refund or inspection result. Keep the approved scope and completion information for your records.

Pre-sale repairs without concealing the cause

Pre-sale maintenance can group suitable doors, skirting, fittings and wall-finish repairs before photography or an open home. Cosmetic work and investigation of an underlying defect need separate decisions.

Prioritise safety, active damage and reliable operation before appearance. Ellis scopes accepted repairs and finish work, with any investigation identified separately. A sale deadline does not justify covering a continuing leak or structural concern.

For doors that rub, damaged skirting and wall marks before photography, note the room, symptom and deadline. Identify recurring staining or movement rather than describing every item as a cosmetic touch-up. The accepted work and timing are confirmed directly.

Cosmetic work and hidden causes

A scuff or isolated impact can be a finish repair, while recurring cracks, dampness or movement may point to an underlying fault. The history and condition need assessment. Painting or covering the symptom does not resolve or establish the absence of a defect.

A practical priority list

A pre-sale list should record each item, safety or active-damage concerns, the desired finish, access and the photography or inspection date. Separate necessary investigation from optional appearance work so the quote can reflect priorities without promising a sale result.

Common-property repairs and safety-rated doors

Approved strata maintenance can include suitable hallway trim, noticeboards and non-electric fittings. The precise lot or common-property location and the authorised approval contact must be identified before work is agreed. Agree any completion photos and summaries with the responsible contact as part of the accepted scope; they are not an automatic reporting package.

An occupant requesting attendance does not establish authority for common-property changes. Confirm the approved list, access restrictions and variation process with the responsible contact. Fire-rated doors and other safety systems are separate specialist scopes.

Loose hallway skirting and a noticeboard repair may be ordinary fittings work, while a shared fire door that fails to close needs its safety requirements assessed. Do not alter rated hardware as a convenient small repair.

Common-property authority

The person authorised for the particular common-property work must approve the scope and any variation. Roles depend on the property’s arrangements and should be confirmed with its responsible manager. An individual resident’s access or request alone is insufficient.

Fire-rated door requirements

A fire-rated door is a safety system whose leaf, frame, closer, latch and other components work together. Ordinary hinge or hardware changes can affect that system. Appropriate personnel must check the product and building requirements before modification.

Office and shop maintenance around occupants

Small commercial maintenance can include suitable office furniture, whiteboards, shop fittings and wall patches. Access, public areas and responsibility for approval matter as much as the item needing repair.

Describe opening hours and your preferred work window, including an after-hours request. Ellis confirms the appointment and any restrictions directly. Electrical, fire-safety, security and structural tasks need separate appropriate own-team scopes rather than a general fittings booking.

Office desk assembly and whiteboard hanging can be listed together. A retail wall left damaged after shelves are removed needs backing and finish checks. Agree occupant protection, noisy work, access and making good before attendance.

Specialist workplace systems

Fixed electrical work, plumbing, gas, security systems, fire-safety equipment and structural changes need the appropriate qualified personnel. Working in a small office does not make those tasks ordinary minor repairs. Ellis separates the regulated scope from furniture and fittings work.

Workplace access details

An office should identify the approval contact, reception or key arrangements, parking, lifts, working hours and occupied areas. Mention restricted rooms, noisy-work limits and protection requirements. Access instructions should be shared privately with the people arranging the visit.

Detailed property maintenance questions

What can a property maintenance list include?

List the fittings, doors, surfaces and outdoor items needing attention. Ellis assesses each task and identifies specialist requirements. The agreed quote defines what is included, any exclusions and the completion information you need.

How often should minor maintenance be checked?

Use the property condition, exposure and fault history to plan a review interval. Tell Ellis about new damage or recurring problems when they appear. A routine schedule should not delay assessment of an unstable or unsafe item.

What photos are useful in a rental maintenance record?

Where agreed, record the affected item, its location and relevant before-and-after condition, with notes for unfinished or separately approved work. Avoid occupants and private information. Photos do not establish concealed construction or replace the approved scope.

Who should authorise rental repairs?

Confirm the landlord, property manager or other authorised contact for the particular work. A tenant reporting a fault or opening the property does not automatically approve repairs or variations. Ellis agrees the approval and access roles before proceeding.

Should I get approval before repairing rental damage?

Confirm the scope and permission with the authorised property contact before changing fittings or finishes. Share the approved list with Ellis and identify who approves variations. Access alone does not establish permission for repairs.

Does a repair guarantee that my bond will be returned?

No. Ellis agrees and completes a repair scope, while bond and inspection decisions involve matters outside that work. Keep approval and completion records and discuss any dispute with the responsible property contact or appropriate advice service.

How are cosmetic repairs separated from underlying defects?

Tell Ellis whether the issue is isolated, recurring or changing and mention moisture or movement. Surface appearance alone is insufficient. Underlying causes need assessment before patching or painting is chosen.

What helps prioritise repairs before selling?

Record each fault, whether it is active or unsafe, the desired finish and your deadline. Ellis assesses the work and separates investigation from cosmetic repairs. Agree priorities and timing directly; no sale outcome is promised.

Who approves repairs on strata common property?

Confirm the person authorised for that particular work through the responsible property contact. Ellis agrees the scope, access and variation approvals before proceeding. A resident’s request alone does not establish common-property authority.

Are fire-door repairs ordinary handyman work?

No ordinary door adjustment should be assumed. Fire-rated doors need their complete product and building requirements checked by appropriate personnel. Tell Ellis about labels and closing faults so a specialist scope is assessed before changes.

Which commercial maintenance tasks need specialist trades?

Electrical, plumbing, gas, security, fire-safety and structural work need their appropriate assessment and qualifications. Ellis identifies its own suitable personnel and agrees the scopes separately from furniture assembly or minor finish repairs.

What access information should an office provide?

Give the approval contact, entry or key arrangements, parking, lifts, working hours and occupied-area restrictions. Discuss noisy work and protection needs. Ellis confirms the appointment and access directly; keep sensitive instructions private.
