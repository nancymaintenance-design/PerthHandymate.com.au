01

Our service plans

Choose the arrangement that fits your property. Each plan starts with an agreed task list; repeat visits are arranged directly with Ellis.

One-off home repairs

Group sticking doors, loose fittings and wall repairs by room and priority. We assess which jobs can be completed together and which need a separate specialist scope.

Rental, vacate and pre-sale work

Use the approved inspection or repair list and tell us the handover or photography date. Prioritise safety, active damage and reliable operation before appearance. Repairs and cleaning are separate; accepted work and timing are confirmed directly.

Repeat property maintenance

Discuss the property's condition, past faults and preferred interval. We confirm suitable tasks, approvals and visit arrangements; an enquiry does not establish ongoing attendance.

Remote owners and multiple properties

Nominate a local access contact and the person who approves quotes and variations. Keep each address, fault list and approval record distinct. Agree any completion photos or summaries before booking.

Choosing a maintenance interval

Use property condition, exposure and repair history to plan reviews. Report new damage and recurring faults when they appear. An unstable or unsafe item needs assessment before the next routine visit.

02

Included services

These are tasks we can assess for your list. The written quote confirms the accepted work, materials and exclusions for your property.

Doors, cupboards and fittings

Sticking doors, cupboard hinges, damaged hardware and suitable indoor fittings. The support, condition and fixing method are checked before repair.

Walls, trim and screens

Approved nail-hole patches, local wall damage, skirting and flyscreen mesh. Confirm any patching and paint finish in the quote. Recurring cracks, dampness or movement need assessment before a cosmetic finish is chosen.

Outdoor maintenance items

Accessible outdoor fittings and exposed timber can be listed for assessment. Describe weathering, looseness and previous repairs so the quote addresses the actual condition.

Strata, office and shop fittings

Suitable hallway trim, noticeboards, desk assembly, whiteboard hanging and minor shop-fitting repairs. Wall patches after shelf removal need backing and finish checks. Confirm the lot or common-property location, approval contact and occupied-area restrictions.

Specialist systems and fire-rated doors

Electrical, plumbing, gas, security, fire-safety and structural tasks need their appropriate assessment and qualifications. Ellis separates these scopes from minor fittings work. Fire-rated doors require product and building requirements to be checked before changes to the leaf, frame, closer or latch.

What a maintenance list does not establish

A defined repair list is not an unlimited package or an inspection guarantee. Surface repairs do not establish the absence of concealed defects. Vacate repairs do not guarantee a bond refund or inspection result; pre-sale repairs do not promise a sale outcome.

03

Our work process

  1. Tell us the tasks

    Share the suburb or postcode, affected rooms and priorities. Mention previous repairs, recurring faults and any deadline. Photos are optional; we can assess on site.

  2. Confirm authority and access

    Identify who approves the quote and variations. Agree the tenant appointment, key collection and return, parking, lifts and working-hour restrictions privately with the responsible contact.

  3. Assess and agree the quote

    We check the condition, materials and specialist requirements, then set out the accepted tasks and exclusions in writing. Confirm the scope and price basis before work proceeds.

  4. Complete the agreed repairs

    Work follows the approved list. Extra damage or changed materials are discussed for approval before additional work. Agree the completion summary or photos you need for handover.

Tenant access and common-property permission

Reporting a fault or opening the property does not authorise repairs or extra spending. Confirm the landlord, property manager or other authorised person for the particular task. Resolve unclear strata responsibility before booking; a resident's request alone is insufficient.

Occupied workplaces and after-hours requests

Tell us about reception, restricted rooms, noisy-work limits and protection around occupants. Request a preferred work window directly; the appointment and building restrictions still need confirmation. Keep sensitive access instructions private.

04

Charging standards

Property maintenance is quoted for the agreed list after assessment. Contact Ellis for your property's written quote.

Your quote basis

The task type, number of repairs, materials, site condition, access and specialist requirements affect the quote. Combining tasks does not automatically establish a package price.

Confirm the included work

Review the accepted tasks, materials and exclusions with the person authorised to approve the work. Confirm any purchase-order requirement and the completion information requested.

Changes need approval

Additional repairs, hidden damage or material changes may alter the scope and price. Ellis discusses the revised work with the authorised contact before proceeding.

05

Aftercare and completed work

Keep a useful completion record

Keep the approved list and agreed summary or before-and-after photos. Record the item, room and any unfinished or separately approved tasks. Photos should avoid occupants, keys and private documents; they do not prove concealed work. For approved strata or common-property repairs, agree completion photos and summaries with the responsible contact as part of the accepted scope; they are not an automatic reporting package.

Contact us about completed repairs

If you have a concern about completed work, contact the Ellis team with the property, repaired item and quote or job details. Explain what has changed and when you noticed it; photos are optional.

Agree any follow-up work

We review the concern against the agreed work and discuss any assessment needed. A recurring symptom, new damage or extra request may need a separate scope; any proposed changes are discussed with the authorised contact.
