Garden Clean Up in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Garden Clean Up?

Garden clean-up helps restore overgrown or untidy outdoor areas through a defined mix of cutting back, clearing and green-waste handling.

  • Cut back manageable overgrowth and remove spent plant material
  • Weed and tidy garden beds, paths and accessible edges
  • Collect and coordinate disposal of agreed green waste
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Property size and photos of each work zone
  • Vegetation height, waste volume and access width
  • Known sharp waste, pests, unstable branches or restricted areas
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Large trees, overhead services, hazardous waste and potentially asbestos-containing debris need specialist assessment rather than routine clean-up.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
