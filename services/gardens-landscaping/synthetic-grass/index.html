Synthetic Grass service guide

Synthetic Grass in Perth: Common Questions

Can synthetic grass be laid over an uneven or poorly draining area?

The base and drainage need assessment first. Excavation and preparation should create a stable, free-draining foundation before the turf is cut, joined and secured.

What details affect the choice and layout of synthetic turf?

Describe the area's size, intended use, sun exposure and existing drainage. Edges, penetrations, joins and infill need to suit the selected system, with underground services checked before excavation.

Synthetic Grass Work We Assess

  • Excavate and prepare a stable, free-draining base
  • Cut, join and secure the chosen synthetic turf system
  • Detail edges, penetrations and infill for the intended use
