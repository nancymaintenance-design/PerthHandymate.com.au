A practical route for garden landscapers

Garden landscaping covers planned changes to planting, beds, edging and outdoor layout rather than routine mowing alone.

Common requests

  • Develop a practical planting and soft-landscape layout
  • Prepare beds, improve soil and install selected plants or mulch
  • Add garden edging and coordinate compatible minor landscape elements

Safety and qualifications

Excavation should account for underground services, drainage and retaining needs; significant structures or level changes may require specialist design.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
