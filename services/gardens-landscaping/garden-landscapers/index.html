Garden Landscapers in Perth: Common Questions

Can existing beds be refreshed without redesigning the whole garden?

A defined area can be scoped for bed preparation, soil improvement, selected planting and mulch. Describe the beds you want to keep and the changes you would like to make.

What needs checking before changing garden levels?

Changes to levels can affect drainage, retaining needs and nearby structures. Underground services must be considered before excavation, and significant level changes may need specialist design.
