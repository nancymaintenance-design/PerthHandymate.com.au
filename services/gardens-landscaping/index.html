Garden Clean Up

Garden clean-up helps restore overgrown or untidy outdoor areas through a defined mix of cutting back, clearing and green-waste handling.

  • Cut back manageable overgrowth and remove spent plant material
  • Weed and tidy garden beds, paths and accessible edges
  • Collect and coordinate disposal of agreed green waste

Garden Landscapers

Garden landscaping covers planned changes to planting, beds, edging and outdoor layout rather than routine mowing alone.

  • Develop a practical planting and soft-landscape layout
  • Prepare beds, improve soil and install selected plants or mulch
  • Add garden edging and coordinate compatible minor landscape elements

Gardeners

Gardening services support recurring or one-off care of established lawns, garden beds, shrubs and small plants.

  • Weed, prune and maintain garden beds within a defined visit
  • Shape manageable shrubs and remove spent growth
  • Refresh mulch and complete seasonal planting or general garden care

Lawn Mowing

Lawn mowing covers routine cutting, edging and presentation of accessible residential or managed-property lawn areas.

  • Mow lawns to an appropriate height for the turf and season
  • Edge paths, garden borders and accessible fixed features
  • Collect or mulch clippings according to the agreed service

Outdoor

Synthetic Grass

Synthetic-grass services cover preparation and installation for lawns, courtyards, play spaces or other suitable outdoor areas.

  • Excavate and prepare a stable, free-draining base
  • Cut, join and secure the chosen synthetic turf system
  • Detail edges, penetrations and infill for the intended use
Explore service

Outdoor

Tree Arborists

Arborist services address tree health, risk, pruning and removal decisions where tree size, condition or location needs specialist knowledge.

  • Assess tree health, defects and risks to people or property
  • Prune trees using species-appropriate and structurally sound methods
  • Plan tree removal, stump handling or clearance where assessment supports it
