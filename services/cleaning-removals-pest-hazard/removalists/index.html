Furniture & Household Removalists service guide

Furniture & Household Removalists in Perth: Common Questions

Can a move include furniture disassembly and reassembly?

List the furniture and the assembly tasks when requesting the move scope. Packing, disassembly and reassembly are included only where agreed, with access and item condition considered.

What access details are needed at each address?

Tell Ellis about stairs, lifts, parking, doorway widths and vehicle access at both pickup and delivery. Declare pianos, safes and oversized or fragile pieces because they may need specialist equipment.

Furniture & Household Removalists Work We Assess

  • Move listed furniture and boxes between agreed addresses
  • Provide packing, disassembly or reassembly where included in the scope
  • Handle bulky or fragile items using planned access and protection

Furniture & Household Removalists: On-site Checks & Optional Details

  • Pickup and delivery suburbs, dates and access conditions
  • Inventory, approximate box count and heavy or fragile items
  • Stairs, lifts, parking, vehicle access and furniture assembly needed at pickup or delivery

Pianos, safes, oversized items and hazardous goods require disclosure and may need specialist equipment or separate arrangements.

Send the inventory, both access descriptions and preferred date for a removal scope.
