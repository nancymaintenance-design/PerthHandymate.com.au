Rubbish Removal service guide

Rubbish Removal in Perth: Common Questions

Can household rubbish and green waste be collected together?

List the materials and approximate quantities so the accepted load and disposal arrangements can be confirmed. Renovation debris and items needing separate disposal should be identified individually.

What materials must be declared before rubbish collection?

Declare asbestos, chemicals, batteries, gas cylinders, sharps and contaminated waste before collection is scoped. Leave suspect material undisturbed and keep it separate from ordinary rubbish. Suspect asbestos assessment and removal is a distinct service with controlled handling and disposal requirements.

What helps estimate the collection scope?

Describe the complete item list, approximate pile volume and heavy pieces, along with access width, stairs and parking. Photos are optional and should only be taken without disturbing suspect material.
