Cleaners in Perth: Common Questions

What should be included in an end-of-lease cleaning brief?

Provide the property size, room count and requested checklist, including priority kitchen and bathroom tasks. Surface materials, current condition and access help define the agreed clean.

Can routine cleaners remove asbestos dust or hazardous residue after building work?

These hazards need specialist assessment and handling. Disclose suspected asbestos dust, sharps, mould or chemical spills before a post-project clean; a general cleaning scope does not cover uncontrolled hazards.

Cleaners Work We Assess

  • Complete an agreed room-by-room general clean

Cleaners: On-site Checks & Optional Details
