Asbestos Removal service guide

Asbestos Removal in Perth: Common Questions

Can an old fibre cement fence be repaired before it is checked for asbestos?

Leave suspect panels undisturbed until a competent asbestos professional has assessed the material. Do not drill, cut, sand or break it to prepare for a fence repair.

What information helps scope asbestos removal?

Describe the material's location, approximate area and condition, along with the building age and any existing laboratory report. Isolation, removal, disposal and clearance needs depend on the assessed material and job.

Should I clean up pieces from a damaged suspect asbestos sheet?

Keep people away and leave the pieces in place. Do not sweep or vacuum them. The assessment must establish suitable controls and appropriately licensed handling where required.
