Verandah Builders in Perth: Common Questions

Can damaged verandah boards or roof sheets be replaced?

Selected components can be included in a repair scope after the surrounding structure is checked. Deteriorated posts or connections may require structural work rather than a surface replacement.

What needs checking before a verandah is attached to the house?

The house construction, roof connection, footings and stormwater arrangement need review. Provide any existing drawings; design, approvals and qualified building work may be required.
