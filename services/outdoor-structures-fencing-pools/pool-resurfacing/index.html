Pool Resurfacing in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Pool Resurfacing?

Pool resurfacing renews a deteriorated interior finish after the shell, leaks, equipment interfaces and chosen coating system are assessed.

  • Inspect the pool surface for delamination, staining and cracking
  • Prepare the shell for a compatible render, pebble or coating system
  • Apply the selected finish and coordinate curing and refill requirements
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Pool dimensions, construction type and approximate age
  • Photos of staining, cracks, loose areas and fittings
  • Water-loss history, previous coatings and access for equipment
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Draining a pool can affect shell stability and must be planned by experienced providers; chemicals and confined work areas require controlled handling.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
