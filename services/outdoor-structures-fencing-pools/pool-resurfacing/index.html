Pool Resurfacing in Perth: Common Questions

Can resurfacing cover cracks in a pool shell?

Cracking needs assessment before a finish is selected. A new surface cannot be assumed to resolve a shell defect; the preparation and repair requirements depend on the underlying condition.

Should I drain the pool before the assessment?

Do not drain it as a preparation step without professional advice. Emptying a pool can affect shell stability, so draining, curing and refilling need a planned sequence with experienced personnel.

Pool Resurfacing Work We Assess

  • Inspect the pool surface for delamination, staining and cracking
  • Prepare the shell for a compatible render, pebble or coating system
