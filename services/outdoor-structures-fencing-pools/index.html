Pool Fence Installers

Pool-fence services focus on compliant barrier layouts, gates and repairs intended to maintain safe separation around a pool area.

  • Measure and install a suitable pool-barrier system

  • Repair panels, posts or gates after assessing the complete barrier

Pool Resurfacing

Pool resurfacing renews a deteriorated interior finish after the shell, leaks, equipment interfaces and chosen coating system are assessed.

  • Inspect the pool surface for delamination, staining and cracking
  • Prepare the shell for a compatible render, pebble or coating system
  • Apply the selected finish and coordinate curing and refill requirements
