Retaining Walls in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Retaining Walls?

Retaining-wall services cover replacement or new structures intended to hold soil where height, drainage, loads and boundaries are understood.

  • Assess levels, soil, drainage and loads behind the proposed wall
  • Construct an approved timber, masonry, concrete or modular system
  • Replace a leaning or failed wall after the cause is investigated
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the appropriate pathway and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Wall length, maximum height and level difference
  • Photos of both sides, drainage and nearby buildings or fences
  • Boundary information, material preference and any engineering
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Retaining walls can affect buildings, boundaries and soil stability; engineering, approvals and underground-service checks may be required before excavation.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
