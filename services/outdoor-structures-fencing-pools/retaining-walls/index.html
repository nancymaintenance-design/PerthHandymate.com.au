Retaining Walls in Perth: Common Questions

Can a leaning retaining wall be replaced with the same type?

The reason for the movement needs investigation first. Drainage, soil conditions and loads behind the wall may change the suitable replacement design, even when the original material is preferred.

What details help assess a retaining wall project?

Describe its length, maximum height and the level difference, plus nearby buildings, fences and boundaries. Engineering, approvals and underground-service checks may be required before excavation.
