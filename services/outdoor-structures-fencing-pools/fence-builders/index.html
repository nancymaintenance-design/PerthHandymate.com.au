A practical route for fence builders

Fence-building enquiries cover new boundary or internal fences, replacement runs, gates and common materials such as timber, metal or composite systems.

Common requests

  • Measure the fence line and confirm levels and access
  • Install posts, rails, panels and gates to the selected system
  • Replace a failed fence run after reviewing footings and ground conditions

Safety and qualifications

Boundary position, pool proximity, retaining loads, underground services and local rules may affect the work; do not assume an existing fence marks the legal boundary.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
