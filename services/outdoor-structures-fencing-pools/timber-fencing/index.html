Timber Fencing in Perth: Common Questions

Can loose or damaged timber palings be replaced individually?

Individual boards can form part of the repair scope when the posts and rails remain sound. Rot or storm damage in the supporting sections may require more than new palings.

Can a sagging timber side gate be repaired without replacement?

The hinges, gate frame, post and latch alignment need checking first. Adjustment may suit sound components, while decay or movement can require replacement of the affected support or gate parts.

Why does a timber fence keep leaning after a repair?

Replacing visible boards may leave a failed post, footing or ground issue unresolved. The assessment should check the support and any retaining function before deciding on another local repair.
