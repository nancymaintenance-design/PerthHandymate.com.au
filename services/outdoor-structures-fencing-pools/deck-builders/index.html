Deck Builders in Perth: Common Questions

Can a few rotten deck boards be replaced without rebuilding the deck?

A local board replacement may be suitable if the supporting frame is sound. The assessment checks supports, fixings and the extent of decay before deciding whether a larger repair is needed.

When does a deck repair need structural assessment?

Movement, deteriorated supports, loose balustrades or problems at the house connection need more than a board repair. Keep people off unsafe areas; elevated decks may need engineering and approvals.

What details help plan a new deck?

Describe the intended use, approximate footprint, height above ground and preferred timber or composite material. Site levels, access, stairs and the house connection help define the build scope.
