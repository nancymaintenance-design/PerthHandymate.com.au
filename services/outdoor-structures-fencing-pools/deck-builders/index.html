Deck repairs and maintenance can include suitable local board replacement, fixing repairs and surface preparation. New deck construction is a different scope from retaining a sound existing platform.

Deck Builders: Common Questions

Can a few rotten deck boards be replaced without rebuilding the deck?

A local board replacement may be suitable if the supporting frame is sound. The assessment checks supports, fixings and the extent of decay before deciding whether a larger repair is needed.

What details help plan a new deck?

Describe the intended use, approximate footprint, height above ground and preferred timber or composite material. Site levels, access, stairs and the house connection help define the build scope.

Detailed deck repairs, maintenance & deck building questions

When does a deck repair need structural assessment?

Movement, decayed supports, loose balustrades or affected house connections need more than a board repair. Keep people off unsafe areas. Ellis identifies the appropriate qualified assessment and any engineering or approval requirements for the scope.

Should damaged boards be addressed before oiling?

Yes. Assess loose or damaged boards and fixings first. Oiling cannot restore structural support or stop a loose board moving. Ellis agrees the necessary repairs before the surface-preparation and coating scope.
