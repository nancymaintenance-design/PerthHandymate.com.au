Fencing Contractors in Perth: Common Questions

What helps scope a fence project with several gates and sections?

Outline each fence run, the purpose of the fencing and where pedestrian or vehicle access is needed. The layout assessment considers gate positions, levels and connections to walls or landscaping.

Can fencing beside a pool or retaining wall use a standard layout?

Those interfaces need specific assessment. Pool barriers, retaining loads, boundaries and nearby services can affect the design and require surveys, approvals or specialist input.
