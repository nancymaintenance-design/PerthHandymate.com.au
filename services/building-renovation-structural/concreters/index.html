Concreters in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Concreters?

Concreting services cover slabs, paths, driveways, footings and other formed concrete work designed for the intended load and site.

  • Excavate, form and prepare reinforcement for an agreed concrete element
  • Place and finish concrete to the specified level, fall and texture
  • Saw-cut or detail joints and interfaces after placement
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Dimensions, thickness or intended use and desired finish
  • Photos of access, levels, drainage and adjoining surfaces
  • Plans, reinforcement details and known underground services
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Excavation, structural loads, drainage and underground services must be assessed; engineered slabs or footings require the relevant design and inspections.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
