Concreters in Perth: Common Questions

What needs deciding before a concrete area is poured?

Define the area's use, levels, drainage falls and desired texture. Excavation, formwork, reinforcement and joints need to suit the agreed concrete element and adjoining surfaces.

Can a structural slab or footing be treated as ordinary flatwork?

Structural loads require the relevant design and inspection arrangements. Underground services and excavation conditions must also be assessed before preparation or placement begins.
