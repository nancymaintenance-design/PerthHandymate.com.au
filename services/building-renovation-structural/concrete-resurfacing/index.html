Concrete Resurfacing service guide

Concrete Resurfacing in Perth: Common Questions

Can cracked concrete be resurfaced?

The cracks need assessment first. A cosmetic overlay may suit a sound slab, but moving concrete, major structural cracks or moisture problems need their cause addressed before resurfacing.

What should I tell you about the concrete area?

Describe its size, current coating, stains and intended use, including vehicle traffic. Note drainage and adjoining levels so the preparation and selected finish can be assessed.

Concrete Resurfacing Work We Assess

  • Assess cracking, contamination, moisture and existing coatings
  • Prepare the surface by suitable cleaning or mechanical methods
  • Apply a selected resurfacing system and finish to the agreed area
