Restumping in Perth: Common Questions

Do uneven floors mean the house needs restumping?

Uneven floors are a symptom to assess, not a diagnosis. Accessible stumps, bearers, building levels, drainage and movement history help establish whether support replacement is appropriate.

Should I enter the subfloor to photograph damaged stumps?

Do not enter an unsafe subfloor. Describe what you have noticed and share existing reports or safely taken photos if available. Supporting and lifting the building requires qualified structural planning and controlled work.
