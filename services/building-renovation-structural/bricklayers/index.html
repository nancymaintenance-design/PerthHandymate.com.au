Bricklayers in Perth: Common Questions

Can replacement bricks and mortar match the existing wall?

Brick bond, joint profile and mortar colour can be considered when the repair is scoped. An exact match depends on the existing materials and suitable replacements being available.

Can a cracked or leaning brick wall be rebuilt locally?

The cause and load-bearing role need assessment before a local rebuild is chosen. A leaning wall or structural cracking may require engineering advice, with service and approval checks for excavation.

Bricklayers Work We Assess
