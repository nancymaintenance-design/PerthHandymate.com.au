A practical route for plumbers

Plumbing services can address leaks, blocked fixtures, taps, toilets, pipework and water connections associated with repairs or upgrades.

Common requests

  • Locate and repair leaking accessible pipework or fixtures
  • Clear suitable blocked sinks, toilets or drains after assessment
  • Install or replace taps, toilets and plumbing-connected fixtures

Safety and qualifications

Plumbing work may require an appropriately licensed provider. For uncontrolled water, isolate the supply only if safe and keep clear of electrical equipment or contaminated overflow.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
