Plumbers in Perth: Common Questions

Who should assess a leaking tap or pipe?

Tell Ellis where the leak appears, when it started and any visible damage. Plumbing work may need an appropriately licensed plumber. A qualified assessment confirms the cause and suitable repair or replacement scope.

Can a blocked sink, toilet or drain be assessed?

Describe the affected fixtures and whether there is overflow. The assessment can establish the blockage and suitable clearing work. Keep clear of contaminated overflow and any electrical equipment affected by water.

Is a shower leak always a plumbing repair?

A shower leak may involve a fixture, pipework or the wet-area construction. Tell Ellis where water appears and whether earlier repairs changed it. An appropriately qualified assessment is needed before deciding on plumbing, resealing or a wider wet-area repair.
