Gas Fitters in Perth: Common Questions

What information helps with a gas appliance replacement?

For non-emergency work, tell Ellis the appliance type, brand, model and location. An appropriately qualified assessment checks compatibility and the connection, replacement or disconnection scope before a quote is confirmed.

Can gas pipework be assessed when changing an appliance location?

Share the proposed appliance location and any recent work with Ellis. Gas pipework changes require an appropriately licensed gas fitter where required, with testing and work confirmed within the assessed scope.

Should I book a routine assessment if I suspect a gas leak?

A suspected gas leak needs an urgent safety response. Avoid flames and electrical switching, leave the area and contact the gas emergency service. Call emergency services for immediate danger. Routine enquiries with Ellis are for non-emergency work.
