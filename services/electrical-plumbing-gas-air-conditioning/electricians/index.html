Assessment details for electrical repairs & electrician services

Mixed repairs need scope-specific personnel

A power-point replacement and new fixed wiring belong to electrical work, while a tap or leaking sink belongs to plumbing assessment. Ellis’s own appropriately licensed personnel complete the regulated scope; a handyman name alone establishes no qualification.

A TV bracket plus a new outlet needs mechanical and electrical work assessed separately. See WA electrical-work guidance. For prescribed security equipment, the applicable class and work must also be checked against WA security licence classes; ordinary door handles are not all the same security task.

A cabinet damaged beside a leaking sink needs the plumbing cause assessed as well as the cabinetry. Tell Ellis about both faults. Repairing a panel while water continues to enter may leave the cause unresolved; each accepted trade task is identified in the quote.

Business names and qualifications

A handyman business name does not establish plumbing or electrical licensing. The person and work scope must meet the relevant requirements. Ellis identifies its own appropriately licensed personnel for regulated tasks rather than treating all small-looking repairs as unregulated.

Security work has its own classes

Prescribed security equipment, relevant locks and security doors can involve specific security licence classes. Check the actual work against WA security licence classes. Do not assume every ordinary door handle is regulated security equipment, or that a handyman description authorises all security work.

Tap and power-point requests

Replacing a power point is electrical work; a tap or water-service fault needs the appropriate plumbing scope. Ellis assigns its own appropriately licensed personnel and quotes the tasks separately from minor repairs. See WA electrical-work guidance for fixed-electrical requirements.

Electricians: Common Questions

Who should replace a power point or install a new light?

Fixed electrical work requires an appropriately licensed electrician where required. Tell Ellis the fitting or power point location and the change you want so the electrical assessment can confirm the work scope.

What should I tell Ellis about a circuit that keeps tripping?

Describe what stopped working, when the problem began and the rooms or fittings involved. An appropriately qualified assessment can investigate the fault. Keep clear of exposed wires, smoke, sparks or wet electrical equipment; use emergency services for immediate danger.

Can electrical work be assessed as part of a renovation?

Share the proposed layout, lighting, power point and appliance requirements with Ellis. The assessment can review switchboard and safety-switch needs and confirm the electrical scope before work is agreed.

Detailed electrical repairs & electrician services questions

Does a handyman business name prove electrical or plumbing licensing?

No. Qualifications must be appropriate to the person and proposed work. Ellis identifies its own appropriately licensed personnel for regulated tasks. Ask about the relevant scope and details rather than relying on a general business label.

Do security-door and lock repairs have separate licensing requirements?

They can involve specific security licence classes depending on the equipment and work. See WA security licence classes. Ellis checks the actual product and scope before acceptance; an ordinary handle repair and prescribed security work must not be treated as identical.

Can a general handyman request include taps and power points?

Describe all the faults together, but the regulated tasks need their appropriate own-team personnel and scopes. Ellis separates electrical and plumbing work from ordinary repairs; a general handyman booking does not authorise someone without the relevant qualifications.
