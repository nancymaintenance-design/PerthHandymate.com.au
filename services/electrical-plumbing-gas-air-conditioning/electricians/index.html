A practical route for electricians

Electrical services cover faults, fittings, power points, lighting, switchboards and electrical work associated with renovations or appliances.

Common requests

  • Diagnose tripping circuits, loss of power or faulty fittings
  • Install or replace lights, switches, power points and hard-wired equipment
  • Assess switchboards, safety switches and renovation electrical requirements

Safety and qualifications

Electrical work must be handled by an appropriately licensed electrician where required. Keep clear of exposed wires, heat, smoke, sparks or wet electrical equipment and use emergency services for immediate danger.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
