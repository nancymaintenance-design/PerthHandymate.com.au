Air Conditioning service guide

Air Conditioning in Perth: Common Questions

Can Ellis help with an air conditioner that is not cooling properly?

Tell Ellis which rooms are affected, the system type and any weak airflow, unusual noise or leaks. An appropriately qualified assessment can establish the cause and whether servicing, repair or replacement is suitable.

What information helps with a new air conditioning installation?

Share the property type, rooms you want to heat or cool and any existing system details. The assessment considers room layout, equipment location and access before confirming the installation scope and quote.

Who should assess electrical or refrigerant work on an air conditioner?

Electrical connections and refrigerant work need appropriately qualified trades and relevant licences where required. Tell Ellis the symptoms to arrange an assessment. Keep clear of a unit that is smoking, sparking or visibly damaged; use emergency services for immediate danger.
