What we can coordinate

A practical route for air conditioning

Air conditioning help can cover a new split or ducted system, a poorly performing unit, servicing needs or replacement advice for a home or managed property.

Common requests

  • Assess the room layout, existing system and suitable unit type
  • Install or replace indoor and outdoor equipment where the scope permits
  • Investigate weak airflow, unusual noise, leaks or temperature problems

Safety and qualifications

Electrical connections and refrigerant work may require appropriately licensed providers; isolate a unit that is sparking, smoking or visibly damaged and avoid touching it.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
