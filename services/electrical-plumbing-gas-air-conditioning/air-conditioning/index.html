Air Conditioning service guide

Air Conditioning in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Air Conditioning?

Air conditioning help can cover a new split or ducted system, a poorly performing unit, servicing needs or replacement advice for a home or managed property.

  • Assess the room layout, existing system and suitable unit type
  • Install or replace indoor and outdoor equipment where the scope permits
  • Investigate weak airflow, unusual noise, leaks or temperature problems
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Property type, room sizes and the areas that need heating or cooling
  • Photos and model details of any existing indoor and outdoor units
  • Switchboard location, outdoor-unit access and preferred outcome
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Electrical connections and refrigerant work may require appropriately licensed Ellis team members; isolate a unit that is sparking, smoking or visibly damaged and avoid touching it.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.

Is aircon, air con, split system, cooling, Aircon Installation covered by this service?

These are common ways people describe this type of request. The actual work is assessed by the condition, access, materials involved and the result you want to achieve, rather than by a search term alone.

What can change the recommended approach?

Property condition, access, hidden damage, existing materials and any site-specific safety or approval requirements can affect the suitable next step.

How do I prepare an Air Conditioning service request in Perth?

Send the system type, affected rooms and suburb so Ellis can arrange the air-conditioning assessment, check the system and confirm the service or replacement scope and quote.
