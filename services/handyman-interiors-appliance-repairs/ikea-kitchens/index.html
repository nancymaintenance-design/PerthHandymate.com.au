IKEA Kitchens in Perth: Common Questions

Can you assemble and install an IKEA kitchen I have already ordered?

We can assess installation of a confirmed modular kitchen design. The final plan, order list and delivery status help establish the cabinet inventory and room readiness. The scope covers agreed frames, fronts, panels and fittings once the site requirements are checked.

What needs to be ready before IKEA kitchen cabinets are installed?

The plan and inventory need confirming, along with room dimensions, wall condition and service locations. Tell us about benchtops, appliances, splashbacks and items to retain. Missing parts or unfinished preparation can affect the agreed installation sequence.

Does kitchen cabinet installation include plumbing, gas and electrical connections?

Cabinet installation and regulated connections need their own defined scopes. Appliance and benchtop interfaces are checked during planning. Electrical, gas, plumbing and any applicable structural or waterproofing work require appropriately qualified people and required licences.
