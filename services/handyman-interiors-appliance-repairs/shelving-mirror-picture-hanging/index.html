Shelf Installation, Mirror & Picture Hanging in Perth

A storage shelf, a heavy mirror and a framed picture place different demands on a wall. Ellis checks the item, intended position and accessible support before agreeing suitable fixings and an installation layout.

Arrange an assessment

Shelf installation: wall type and load

Tell Ellis the shelf dimensions, bracket or floating-shelf system and what you intend to store. A decorative shelf is different from storage for heavier items. The wall material, supporting locations and product instructions affect the fixing approach; the visible wall lining alone does not establish suitable support.

We assess the proposed positions and explain any support or wall-condition issue before proceeding. Do not assume a general load figure applies to your wall. The Cockburn shelf installation example shows two shelves installed above a cabinet; photographs do not establish a load rating or concealed fixings.

Mirror hanging and picture placement

For a mirror, share its dimensions, approximate weight if known and the supplied hanging system. A framed mirror, an unframed panel and a small picture may need different supports and handling. We check the condition of the item and agree the position before installation; a heavy mirror is not treated as an ordinary picture hook.

For several pictures, describe the intended grouping, spacing and relationship to nearby furniture. Discuss which marks or existing holes are to remain. Wall patching, paint touch-ups and removal of existing fittings need separate agreement if you want them included.

Wall Shelf Mounting: Shelves, Brackets & Fixings

You can ask Ellis to assess a shelf, mirror or picture-hanging product you already have. Keep the instructions and supplied hardware available. Share the model where possible, but leave further fixing purchases until the wall and product are checked. Supplied hardware may not suit every wall.

The quote identifies who supplies the item, compatible brackets or fixings, any extra support and the installation work. Product assembly can be a separate flat-pack assembly task. Replacement shelves or a custom storage design may require new cabinetry rather than a wall-fixing visit.

Hidden cables, pipes and electrical work

Tell Ellis about known cables, pipes, switches or sockets near the intended fixing position. If the wall material or safe fixing location is uncertain, stop and arrange assessment before disturbance. Suspected asbestos-containing material must remain undisturbed until the appropriate assessment and work plan are confirmed.

Changing a power point or fixed wiring is a separate electrical service completed by Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel. For a television and bracket, use TV wall mounting so screen compatibility, bracket movement and cable arrangements are assessed together.

Agree the layout and request a quote

Living-room shelves above furniture, a hallway mirror and a group of framed pictures each need clearance and placement decisions before drilling. Tell us the number of items, your preferred positions, access restrictions and your suburb. Photos and approximate measurements are optional; an on-site assessment establishes the proposed method and written quote.

For rentals or shared property, confirm permission for wall fixings with the authorised contact. Removal of existing fittings, making good holes, packaging removal and disposal are included only when agreed. What we check on site

Heavy mirrors and picture layouts

Mirror hanging, picture hanging and suitable picture-rail installation require the item and its mounting system checked against the wall. A heavy mirror on brick needs different handling and fixings from a small framed picture on plasterboard.

Share mirror dimensions, approximate weight if known, frame condition and supplied hanging fittings. For a group of pictures, agree spacing, height and which old holes need repair. Suitable support and product condition determine what can be installed.

A large hallway mirror or a gallery wall after moving needs its layout settled around doors and furniture. Plasterboard hooks pulling out indicate a fixing or support issue; reusing the damaged hole without assessment may repeat the problem.

Hooks pulling out

A picture hook can pull out when the load, fixing type or condition of the lining is unsuitable. A damaged hole reduces the options for reuse. The item, mounting system and wall support need assessment together before choosing a new fixing.

Details for a mirror assessment

Mirror dimensions, weight if known, frame or panel type and supplied hanging system help plan the job. Describe the wall, preferred location, access and nearby services. Photos are optional; do not lift an unstable mirror to find hidden labels.

Sagging shelves and practical storage support

Shelf installation includes suitable floating shelves, bracketed shelving and storage systems after the product and support are assessed. Wall construction, bracket spacing, clearances and intended storage affect the arrangement.

A plasterboard wall may need suitable backing or a different position; the lining alone does not establish suitable support. Share the shelf system and intended contents. Ellis agrees extra support, replacement components and any making good in the quote.

Garage storage shelves, apartment shelving and a collapsed pantry shelf place different demands on the installation. A loose bracket or sagging shelf needs both the mounting and the shelf itself checked before it is loaded again.

Why floating shelves sag

Sagging may come from loose brackets, unsuitable support, damaged fixings or a shelf unsuited to the stored load. An apparently level bracket does not establish adequate wall support. Avoid adding more weight while the shelf and wall are assessed.

How shelf loading is assessed

A suitable storage load depends on the complete shelf system, brackets, fixings, supporting wall and manufacturer instructions. The weakest part can limit the installation. A general weight figure or another property’s photograph cannot establish a rating for your shelf.

Shelving, mirror and picture hanging FAQs

Can you install shelves or a mirror I have supplied?

Share the item details, dimensions, instructions and supplied hanging hardware where available. Ellis checks the product and wall before confirming suitable fixings. Any extra brackets, support or wall repairs and who supplies them are identified in the written quote.

Does picture hanging include patching and repainting old holes?

Removal of old fittings, wall patching and paint touch-ups are separate scope items. Tell Ellis which marks or holes need attention and whether matching paint is available. The quote states the agreed preparation and finish work.

Can I request TV mounting or a power point as part of the same enquiry?

You can describe related tasks together, but each needs its own assessment. TV wall mounting includes television and bracket compatibility checks. New or relocated power points and fixed-wiring work are separately scoped with Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel.

Why do picture hooks pull out of plasterboard?

The item load, unsuitable fixings or damaged lining can cause failure. Ellis checks the wall and hanging system rather than placing another hook in the same weakened hole. Heavy items may need a different support arrangement.

What details help you plan mirror installation?

Describe the size, approximate weight if known, frame or panel type and supplied mounting hardware. Include the preferred position, wall material if known and access. Photos are optional; leave an unstable mirror in place for assessment.

Why is my floating shelf sagging?

The shelf, brackets, fixings, wall support and intended load all need checking. Loose or unsuitable components can produce similar sagging. Avoid adding more weight and describe what was stored so Ellis can assess the cause.

How is a suitable shelf load determined?

Ellis considers the product instructions, shelf, brackets, fixings, wall support and intended use together. No general weight figure applies to every installation. Photos alone cannot establish a load rating. Tell us what you plan to store before the support and product are assessed.
