Shelving, Mirror & Picture Hanging in Perth

A storage shelf, a heavy mirror and a framed picture place different demands on a wall. Ellis checks the item, intended position and accessible support before agreeing suitable fixings and an installation layout.

Arrange an assessment

Plan shelves around the wall and intended load

Tell Ellis the shelf dimensions, bracket or floating-shelf system and what you intend to store. A decorative shelf is different from storage for heavier items. The wall material, supporting locations and product instructions affect the fixing approach; the visible wall lining alone does not establish suitable support.

We assess the proposed positions and explain any support or wall-condition issue before proceeding. Do not assume a general load figure applies to your wall. The Cockburn shelf installation example shows two shelves installed above a cabinet; photographs do not establish a load rating or concealed fixings.

Mirror mounting and picture placement

For a mirror, share its dimensions, approximate weight if known and the supplied hanging system. A framed mirror, an unframed panel and a small picture may need different supports and handling. We check the condition of the item and agree the position before installation; a heavy mirror is not treated as an ordinary picture hook.

For several pictures, describe the intended grouping, spacing and relationship to nearby furniture. Discuss which marks or existing holes are to remain. Wall patching, paint touch-ups and removal of existing fittings need separate agreement if you want them included.

Supplied shelves, brackets and wall fixings

You can ask Ellis to assess a shelf, mirror or picture-hanging product you already have. Keep the instructions and supplied hardware available. Share the model where possible, but leave further fixing purchases until the wall and product are checked. Supplied hardware may not suit every wall.

The quote identifies who supplies the item, compatible brackets or fixings, any extra support and the installation work. Product assembly can be a separate flat-pack assembly task. Replacement shelves or a custom storage design may require new cabinetry rather than a wall-fixing visit.

Concealed services and electrical boundaries

Tell Ellis about known cables, pipes, switches or sockets near the intended fixing position. If the wall material or safe fixing location is uncertain, stop and arrange assessment before disturbance. Suspected asbestos-containing material must remain undisturbed until the appropriate assessment and work plan are confirmed.

Changing a power point or fixed wiring is a separate electrical service completed by Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel. For a television and bracket, use TV wall mounting so screen compatibility, bracket movement and cable arrangements are assessed together.

Agree the layout and request a quote

Living-room shelves above furniture, a hallway mirror and a group of framed pictures each need clearance and placement decisions before drilling. Tell us the number of items, your preferred positions, access restrictions and your suburb. Photos and approximate measurements are optional; an on-site assessment establishes the proposed method and written quote.

For rentals or shared property, confirm permission for wall fixings with the authorised contact. Removal of existing fittings, making good holes, packaging removal and disposal are included only when agreed. Read how to describe the work clearly and qualified-work boundaries before planning related alterations.

Shelving, Mirror & Picture Hanging questions

Can you install shelves or a mirror I have supplied?

Share the item details, dimensions, instructions and supplied hanging hardware where available. Ellis checks the product and wall before confirming suitable fixings. Any extra brackets, support or wall repairs and who supplies them are identified in the written quote.

How much weight will the installed shelf hold?

That depends on the shelf system, its brackets and fixings, the supporting wall and the intended use. Photos alone cannot establish a load rating. Tell Ellis what you plan to store so the product instructions and actual support can be assessed before installation.

Does picture hanging include patching and repainting old holes?

Removal of old fittings, wall patching and paint touch-ups are separate scope items. Tell Ellis which marks or holes need attention and whether matching paint is available. The quote states the agreed preparation and finish work.

Can a TV or a power point be included in the same request?

You can describe related tasks together, but each needs its own assessment. TV wall mounting includes television and bracket compatibility checks. New or relocated power points and fixed-wiring work are separately scoped with Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel.
