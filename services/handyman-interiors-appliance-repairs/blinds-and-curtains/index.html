Assessment details for blind installation & curtain repairs

Loose curtain brackets and supplied blinds

Curtain rod installation and non-electric blind fitting require compatible brackets and suitable support. Repairs can address a loose rail or broken roller-blind bracket after the product, fixing holes and surrounding material are checked.

For blinds already purchased, share the model and instructions before more parts are bought. Recess or face mounting, opening dimensions, clearances and window hardware affect fit. Fixed electrical connections for motorised products are a separate electrical scope.

A curtain rod pulled from plasterboard or a rental rail coming loose needs the damaged wall assessed, not just a larger screw. Tell Ellis which brackets failed and confirm permission; replacement roller-blind brackets must match the product.

Recurring loose rails

A rail can loosen when brackets are poorly supported, fixings or holes fail, or the curtain load pulls on unsuitable material. The wall behind the bracket matters. Retightening the same failed fixing does not establish a lasting repair.

Measure for the mounting style

Blind fitting needs opening width and height, recess depth or face-mount positions, and clearance for handles and operation. The product instructions determine which dimensions matter. Ellis measures on site; approximate dimensions and existing photos are optional.

Blinds & Curtains service guide

Blinds & Curtains: Common Questions

Can you install blinds or curtain rods I have already bought?

Yes, after checking the product, opening size and mounting surface. Tell us the style, number of windows and whether brackets are included. Ellis measures on site; photos and measurements are optional. Powered products may also need electrical installation.

Can a curtain rail pulling away from plasterboard be repaired?

The brackets, fixing points and damaged wall area need checking before the rail is refitted. The repair depends on the wall condition and the weight of the curtains. Avoid using a rail that is coming away from the wall until it has been assessed.

What needs checking before replacing broken roller blind brackets?

The blind model, bracket shape and fixing surface determine which replacement will fit. Corded blinds also need suitable child-safety arrangements. Fixed electrical connections for powered blinds require an appropriately licensed electrician.

Detailed blind installation & curtain repairs questions

Why does a curtain rail keep coming loose?

Wall support, fixing condition, bracket arrangement and curtain load can all contribute. Ellis checks the surrounding material before agreeing new fixings or making good. Reusing a damaged hole may repeat the failure.

What measurements are needed before blind fitting?

The opening size, recess depth or face-mount position and clearance around handles matter. Ellis checks these against the product instructions on site. Approximate measurements and photos can help but are optional before contacting us.
