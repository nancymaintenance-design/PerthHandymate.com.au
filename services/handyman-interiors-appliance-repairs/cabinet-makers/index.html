Cabinet Makers in Perth: Common Questions

Can you make fitted cabinetry for a kitchen, laundry or wardrobe?

Cabinet-making work can cover custom storage, replacement units, doors, drawers and panels for an agreed layout. We measure the space and confirm materials, hardware and items to retain. Appliance dimensions and service locations are checked before the specification is finalised.

Does a dropped cupboard door mean the whole cabinet needs replacing?

Not necessarily. Cupboard hinge and drawer-runner repairs may suit sound cabinetry. Ellis checks the hinge, fixing points and cabinet panel; pulled screws, swelling or damage to the carcass can change the repair. New fitted storage and replacement cabinetry have their own measured scope.

Can existing cabinetry be altered for a different dishwasher or oven?

Cabinet alterations depend on the appliance dimensions, available space and condition of the existing units. Provide the new model details and items you want to retain. Electrical, gas or plumbing changes require the relevant licensed trades and are checked as part of the project scope.
