What we can coordinate

A practical route for cabinet makers

Cabinet-making services cover custom storage, replacement cabinetry, alterations and fitted units designed for a specific room.

Common requests

  • Measure and design cabinetry for kitchens, laundries, wardrobes or living areas
  • Manufacture and install selected doors, drawers, carcasses and panels
  • Modify existing cabinetry for changed appliances or storage needs

Safety and qualifications

Cabinet changes around electrical, gas or plumbing services require the relevant licensed trades; wall fixing must account for concealed services.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
