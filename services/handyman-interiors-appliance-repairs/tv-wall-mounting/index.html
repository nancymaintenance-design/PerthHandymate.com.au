TV Wall Mounting in Perth

Ellis Perth assesses the TV, bracket and supporting wall before agreeing a wall-mount installation. Discuss your viewing position and cable arrangement with us; any new power point or fixed electrical work is scoped separately with Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel.

Discuss tv wall mounting

TV and bracket compatibility

Tell us the TV make and model, approximate screen size and whether you already have a bracket. The mounting pattern, television weight and bracket capacity need to suit one another. Fixed, tilting and moving brackets place different demands on the support and surrounding clearance.

Keep the product instructions and mounting hardware available if you have them. We check the supplied bracket before fitting and identify any extra materials in the quote. Owning a bracket does not establish that it is suitable for the TV or wall.

Supporting wall and viewing position

Plasterboard, masonry and other walls require different fixings and support checks. Ellis assesses the intended fixing points and accessible wall structure before confirming an installation method. If support or the wall condition is unsuitable, we explain the options before proceeding.

Discuss the screen height, seating position, glare, doors and furniture around the TV. Moving brackets need clearance through their range of motion. For rentals or strata properties, confirm permission for the proposed wall fixings and any alterations with the authorised contact.

Existing sockets, cables and electrical work

Agree the cable arrangement

Show us where the existing power point and signal connections are, and explain which devices will connect to the TV. We discuss accessible cable placement or a suitable surface cable-cover option. Concealed cable placement depends on the wall construction, access and the type of cable; it is not assumed to be included.

Fixed wiring uses licensed Ellis personnel

Mounting a bracket does not include changing a power point or fixed wiring. Any required electrical work is assessed and quoted separately, and is completed by Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel. See our electrical service for that scope. Do not alter the electrical installation to prepare for a mounting visit.

Installation checks and quotation

The TV and bracket, wall support, height, access and cable arrangement affect the quote. Tell us if an existing TV or bracket needs removal and whether wall repairs are required afterwards. Additional support, parts and electrical work need agreement before proceeding.

We check the mounted TV, bracket operation and the agreed cable arrangement at completion, and explain any remaining scope. Photos and measurements are optional; your suburb and a description of the intended setup are enough to start an enquiry and arrange an on-site assessment.

Shelves, mirrors and pictures have their own load and fixing needs. Include them as separate tasks with our small repair and wall-fixing service rather than assuming a TV bracket quote covers them.

Assessment details for tv wall mounting

Plan television support and power separately

TV wall mounting covers compatible brackets, screen attachment and mechanical fixing after the wall is assessed. Brick and plasterboard walls need different support checks; screen size alone does not establish the required fixing method.

Mounting cost depends on the screen, bracket, wall support, movement clearance and cable arrangement. Using an existing power point differs from adding or relocating one, which is separately scoped with Ellis’s appropriately licensed electrical personnel.

For a large TV on plasterboard or a rental apartment wall, provide the proposed position and bracket details if available. Permission and suitable backing are checked before installation. A new outlet behind the screen needs electrical assessment as well as bracket fitting.

Wall suitability for a television

Not every wall or fixing position is suitable for a television bracket. Screen weight, bracket leverage, wall construction and actual support are considered together. Unknown backing or concealed services must be resolved before selecting fixings.

A new power point is electrical work

Adding a power point behind a television is separate from fitting its bracket. Fixed electrical work requires the appropriate licensed personnel; see WA electrical-work guidance. Ellis scopes the electrical work with its own licensed team and coordinates the mechanical installation.

For related preparation, read what to include in a repair request. Describe your task and suburb; photos are optional. Ellis confirms the work and written quote after an on-site assessment.

TV Wall Mounting questions

Can you use my existing TV bracket?

Ellis checks whether the bracket suits the TV mounting pattern, weight, intended movement and supporting wall. Keep the product instructions and hardware available. Any extra materials or a different bracket are agreed before fitting.

Can you hide the TV cables inside the wall?

We assess the wall, access and cable type before confirming cable placement. Surface cable management may be an option. Concealed cable placement and any fixed electrical changes are separate scope items, not automatically included with bracket installation.

Do I need permission to mount a TV in a rental?

Confirm permission for the wall fixings and any alterations with the authorised property contact before booking. Tell Ellis about building access restrictions and who can approve the work.

Detailed tv wall mounting questions

Can every wall support a mounted TV?

No. Ellis checks screen and bracket compatibility, wall construction, support and the proposed position. A plasterboard surface or a photo alone cannot establish support. Extra backing or a different position may need agreement.

Who adds a power point behind a wall-mounted TV?

Ellis’s own appropriately licensed electrical personnel assess and carry out the agreed electrical scope. Any new or relocated power point needs separate assessment and quotation; bracket fitting does not include fixed wiring by default. See WA electrical-work guidance; agree outlet position, access and making good separately from mechanical mounting.
