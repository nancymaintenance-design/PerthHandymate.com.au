Handymen in Perth: Common Questions

Repairs and work we can assess

  • Complete a grouped list of small household repairs
  • Assemble furniture and fit non-structural accessories
  • Adjust doors, replace basic hardware or complete minor patch-and-fix work
Can I book several small repairs in one request?

Yes. Tell us your suburb and list the jobs you want assessed, such as a sticking door, loose cupboard hinge, damaged trim or fitting that needs securing. We review the tasks together, confirm the work required and provide a written quote.

Can you repair a loose cupboard hinge or a sticking drawer?

We check the hinge or drawer runner, fixing points and surrounding cabinet material. The work can involve alignment, compatible hardware or repairing the fixing area. Tell us if screws keep pulling out, the door hangs unevenly or the drawer catches. Custom cabinetry is a separate service.

Do you assemble flat-pack furniture and fit shelves or brackets?

Furniture assembly and mounting requests are part of our handyman service. Tell us what you need assembled or fitted, and whether the item and its parts are on site. We check the instructions, wall or fixing surface and suitable mounting arrangements before agreeing the work.

Can you help prepare a rental property or a home for sale?

We can assess a practical list of small repairs, including doors, cupboards, trim, fittings and assembly jobs. For a rental, identify the person who can approve repairs and arrange access. We confirm the agreed work rather than promise a particular inspection, bond or sale outcome.

How do you quote for handyman jobs, and what needs a separate trade?

We assess the tasks, materials and access, then provide a written quote for the agreed work. Ask us to clarify materials and any separate services in that quote. Work requiring a qualified trade is identified before it proceeds; ordinary handyman work is not a substitute for regulated work.

Do I need photos, measurements or a complete repair list to get started?

No. Contact Ellis directly with the problem and your suburb. Our team can arrange an on-site assessment and help clarify the work. Any existing photos or list are optional, so you can ask for help even if you are unsure what is causing the problem.

On-site checks and optional job details

Electrical, plumbing, gas, structural, roofing-height and hazardous-material work require appropriately licensed or specialist work rather than general handyman work.

List the tasks and any supplied parts. Ellis assesses the work on site and confirms the handyman repair scope, qualified service arrangements for regulated work and written quote.

If already available, the following details help us discuss the job. They are not required before contacting Ellis.

  • A prioritised task list with a photo of each item
  • Wall, floor or furniture materials and any product instructions
  • Property access, parking and whether parts are already supplied

Cabinet Repairs, Furniture Assembly & Wall Mounting

Cupboard hinges and drawer runners

A cupboard hinge pulled out, stripped fixing holes or a sticking drawer calls for a repair assessment, not automatically a new cabinet. We check hardware, alignment and the cabinet material before agreeing the repair. See our cupboard hinge repair example. For new fitted storage, visit cabinet-making services.

Flat-pack furniture assembly

Tell us whether you need a bed, desk, wardrobe or other flat-pack item assembled. Keep the product instructions and supplied parts available if you have them. We check the assembly and fixing requirements before quoting; kitchen installation is a separate service.

Shelves, mirrors and brackets

Describe the shelf, mirror, picture or TV bracket you want fitted and where it will go. The item weight, wall material, supplied fittings and concealed services affect the fixing method. We inspect the location rather than assume every wall can take the same mounting.

Loose bathroom and household fittings

A loose towel rail, toilet roll holder or other non-structural accessory needs its fixing surface checked. Tell us if the fitting keeps pulling out or sits beside tiles. We confirm the suitable work and any separate trade requirements before proceeding.

Several small jobs in one visit

TV brackets, floating shelves and heavy mirrors

Sliding Door Repairs in Perth

A sliding door that drags, jumps off its track or needs force to close may have worn rollers, a damaged track or alignment problems. Ellis repairs sliding doors, including heavy glass panels, with the handling method and replacement hardware checked on site. We assess the rollers, track, frame and closing action before agreeing the repair.

Can you replace sliding door rollers without replacing the whole door?

Yes, where the panel, frame and track are suitable to retain. We check the roller type, panel weight and track condition before quoting. A worn track or damaged frame may need additional work; a roller change alone is not assumed to solve every fault.

Clothesline Repairs, Installation & Restringing in Perth

Ellis installs, repairs and restrings rotary, folding and retractable clotheslines. We check the frame, line tension, brackets and fixing points when lines sag, snap or no longer retract. For a new clothesline, we assess the mounting surface, usable space and clearance before agreeing the installation.

Can you restring my clothesline or fit one I have already bought?

Yes. Tell us the clothesline type and whether you want new line, a hardware repair or a replacement unit fitted. We check compatibility and the wall or ground fixing location. Product supply, removal of the old unit and any new supports are identified in the quote.

Letterbox Installation & Outdoor Fixture Repairs in Perth

Can you fit a letterbox or hose reel to an existing wall or post?

Can you install a pet door in glass or a security screen?

Laminate & Vinyl Floor Repairs in Perth

We repair local damage to laminate and vinyl flooring, including damaged boards or planks, loose trims and lifting edges. We check the flooring system, matching material and subfloor before agreeing a repair. Swelling, recurring movement or moisture needs attention to the cause as well as the visible damage.

Can you replace a damaged laminate board or vinyl plank?

Yes, where the flooring system and available replacement material allow a local repair. Click-lock boards and glued flooring need different access and preparation. We check how much surrounding flooring must be lifted and whether spare matching material is available before quoting.

Pressure Cleaning for Patios, Paving & Outdoor Areas in Perth

Ellis provides pressure cleaning for suitable paving, patios and outdoor surfaces. We assess the surface, stains, joints, coatings and drainage before selecting the cleaning method. Pressure and cleaning products are chosen for the material to avoid treating every surface in the same way.

Can pressure cleaning be combined with a garden tidy-up?

Yes. List the paved areas and garden tasks you want included so we can assess them together. We confirm access, water supply, runoff and any separate repairs. Suspected asbestos, fragile surfaces and damaged coatings are not treated as routine pressure-cleaning areas.

Grab Rail Installation & Home Repairs for Seniors in Perth

Eaves, Patio & Pergola Repairs in Perth

We repair damaged eaves linings, timber trim and suitable patio or pergola components. Ellis checks the material, supports and cause of damage before agreeing local repairs. Soft timber, sagging components or recurring roof leaks may need a broader repair plan rather than a cosmetic patch.

Can a damaged eaves or pergola section be repaired locally?

Yes, when the surrounding material and support are sound. We inspect water entry, fixings and the affected section before quoting. Suspected asbestos linings are assessed before disturbance, and load-bearing components require the appropriate structural checks.
