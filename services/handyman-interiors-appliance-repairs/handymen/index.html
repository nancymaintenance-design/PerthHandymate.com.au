Cupboard Hinge & Wardrobe Door Repairs

Cabinet hinge repair starts with the hinge, mounting plate and fixing holes, not an order for new cupboards. Ellis checks loose screws, stripped fixings, door alignment and drawer runners, then agrees whether adjustment or compatible hardware will suit the existing cabinet. Tell us which door drops or rubs and whether a previous fixing has failed.

Wardrobe doors need their own checks

For wardrobe door repairs, we check guides, stops, tracks, rollers and clearances. Mirrored panels need their condition and handling assessed before removal; do not force an unstable panel. Wardrobe fittings are different from external glass sliding-door hardware, so identify the affected door when contacting Ellis.

See our cupboard hinge and alignment example and Cupboard hinge and drawer advice →. The example shows a cabinet repair, not wardrobe roller work. Custom or replacement cabinetry is a separate project. For other tasks, use furniture assembly or TV wall mounting.

Shelf Installation & Wall Fixings

Shelf installation is planned around the wall and intended load. Tell Ellis the shelf dimensions, bracket system and what you want it to hold. We check the wall material, supporting locations, concealed-service concerns and compatible fixings before agreeing the layout. Floating shelves and bracketed shelves can need different support; a heavy storage shelf is not treated as a picture hook.

Can I supply shelves and brackets?

Yes. Share the product details and instructions if available, and confirm whether all fixings are included. Ellis checks suitability on site and identifies extra materials or wall repairs in the quote. Photos and approximate measurements are optional. For television brackets use TV wall mounting; for kit furniture use flat-pack assembly.

Internal Door & Sliding Door Repairs

An ordinary hinged door that rubs, drops or will not latch may suit adjustment rather than replacement. Ellis checks hinges, loose handles, the latch strike, frame condition and floor clearance before agreeing the repair. Tell us when the problem began, especially after a flooring change or earlier repair; continuing frame movement needs investigation.

A new door, changed opening or damaged frame is assessed under door installation. For a patio glass panel, use our sliding-door roller and track repair service. Entry, fire-rated and security doors have their own requirements; the quote identifies specialist work rather than treating every door as a routine adjustment.

Clothesline Repairs & Installation

Clothesline repairs can involve restringing or rethreading lines, compatible tensioning parts, arms, brackets and fixings. Ellis checks the frame and support before replacing the visible line: a corroded frame or loose post can change whether repair is worthwhile. Tell us whether the unit is folding, rotary or retractable and which part no longer works.

Repair the existing unit or install a replacement?

For clothesline installation, confirm whether Ellis is quoting supply and installation or installation of your supplied product. We check the product instructions, usable space and wall or ground support. Old-unit removal, disposal, new brackets, posts and support work are included only when agreed in the quote. Leave further part purchases until compatibility is checked.

Pet Door Installation: Dogs & Cats

Pet door installation uses one assessment for dog doors and cat flaps, with the opening suited to the pet and the door construction. Tell Ellis the pet size, product model and whether the panel is timber, ordinary insect-screen mesh or glass. For a suitable timber door we check thickness, framing and the proposed cut-out; on mesh screens we check whether the frame can support the product.

Can a pet door be fitted directly into glass?

The glazing must be assessed before a fitting method is agreed. Do not cut or drill the existing pane yourself; a purpose-made replacement pane may be needed. Glazing work and any security-screen changes have separate product and qualified-work requirements. A pet flap must not be assumed to preserve a screen's security performance.

Existing photos are optional. Ellis checks the opening on site and confirms the product, fitting method, supply and disposal scope before quoting.

Pressure Cleaning for Patios & Paving

Our handyman pressure-cleaning scope is suitable accessible patios and paving. Ellis checks the surface, coating, joints and drainage before selecting a cleaning method; more pressure is not automatically appropriate for a fragile finish. Tell us about flaking coatings, loose pavers, nearby doors and where runoff can go.

Does this include roof or asbestos cleaning?

No. Roof cleaning is outside this patio and paving task, and suspected asbestos is excluded from pressure cleaning. Leave suspect material undisturbed and ask for the appropriate assessment. Surface cleaning also does not repair a failed base or persistent pooling: use paving repair assessment for loose or sunken areas.

Confirm the area to be cleaned, access, water availability and protection or cleanup requirements when Ellis prepares the written quote.

Grab Rail Installation & Support Checks

Grab rail installation needs a suitable product and supporting wall structure, not a fixing into the visible lining alone. Ellis checks the proposed location, wall material, fixing support and access before agreeing installation. Tell us about tiled areas or concealed services and bring any placement plan from your adviser.

Does installation include an accessibility assessment?

Placement advice, accessibility assessment and installation are separate scopes. Ellis confirms what can be fixed at the requested position; this is not an accessibility guarantee or a claim of NDIS registration or funding eligibility. Confirm the person approving the work and any funding requirements separately before booking.

The quote identifies the rail, supply responsibility, fixings and any support or finish work. See Safety-critical repairs and qualifications → for related repair boundaries.

Strata Maintenance & Authorised Access

For strata maintenance, identify the exact lot or common-property location and the person authorised to approve assessment and repairs. An occupant's request to attend does not establish approval for work on common property. Bring the approved task list, spending limit if applicable and the contact for any variation.

Ellis agrees tenant or occupant appointments, keys, parking and shared-area access before attendance. Flag noisy work and any building access rules. Repair findings and extra work go back to the nominated approval contact before proceeding. Use our property maintenance service for the wider repair list and managed-property coordination guide to prepare the authority and access details.

Vacate Repairs & Repair Handover

Vacate repairs address the authorised repair list: for example loose cupboard hardware, damaged wall patches or sticking doors. Bring the property manager's marked list if available and distinguish damage to repair from cleaning tasks. End-of-lease cleaning has its own agreed checklist and quote.

Confirm the approval contact, key collection, access window and planned handover date. Ellis scopes the repairs, materials and desired finish, seeks approval for variations and agrees how completion details will be handed over. Existing photos are optional; do not remove fittings to prepare them. A repair booking does not guarantee a bond refund or an inspection result. See wall patching and finish choices where a vacate list includes plaster damage.

Handyman Booking Questions

Can I book several small repairs in one visit?

Yes. Tell Ellis your suburb and the jobs you want completed. We assess them together and agree the repair list, materials and quote. Grouping compatible tasks lets you organise cupboard repairs, assembly and other small jobs through one contact.

How are handyman jobs quoted?

We provide a written quote after an on-site assessment. Ask whether the job is priced by time or as a booking block, and which labour, materials and disposal costs are included. Additional repairs need their scope and price agreed before proceeding.

Can I supply the furniture, fittings or replacement parts?

Yes. Share the product details and tell us what is already on site. We check compatibility, missing hardware and installation requirements before confirming the work. Product supply and any extra materials are identified in the quote.

Can you work at rentals, strata properties or business premises?

Yes. Confirm the person authorised to approve repairs and arrange entry. Tell us about tenant appointments, common-property approval, parking and trading hours so we can agree access before the visit.

Do I need photos or measurements before contacting Ellis?

No. Describe the problem by phone or through Contact Us and provide your suburb. Photos, measurements and a repair list are optional. Our Perth team checks the property on site and helps define the work.

What happens if my repair needs a specialist trade?

Ellis identifies electrical, plumbing, gas, structural and other specialist requirements during assessment. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. Fixed wiring, wet-area waterproofing and structural changes are separate from ordinary small repairs.
