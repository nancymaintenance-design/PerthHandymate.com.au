Repair details by task

Letterboxes, posts and hose-reel fixings

Letterbox installation and local repairs include checking the post, base, ground support and clearance. A knocked-over letterbox or hose reel pulling away from brickwork needs the whole support assessed. Share the product and visible damage; parts, supply, removal and disposal are agreed separately.

Before ground disturbance

A proposed letterbox post position needs checks for underground services, boundaries, nearby access and the installation requirements. Do not assume an empty-looking patch of ground is clear. Ellis agrees the location and necessary checks before digging.

Weather and outdoor fixings

Weather exposure, corrosion, movement and deteriorating wall material can loosen outdoor fixings. A replacement screw cannot restore damaged support by itself. The fitting, fixing points and surrounding material need checking together before further loading.

Why do outdoor wall fixings loosen over time?

Exposure, corrosion, movement or damaged wall material can weaken the fixing. Ellis checks the product and supporting surface together. Retightening a failed fixing may not address the cause.

Choosing a pet door for your door or panel

Share the pet size and product model. A timber door needs its thickness, framing and cut-out checked; ordinary mesh needs a frame that can support the product. Glazing work and security-screen alterations have separate requirements. A pet flap must not be assumed to preserve security performance.

Toughened glass is not a site cut-out

Do not drill or cut an existing toughened-glass pane. We assess the glass and pet-door product and confirm whether a purpose-made replacement pane is needed before installation.

Preserving security requirements

A pet-door opening can affect a security door’s complete product system and performance. Product approval, installation requirements and relevant qualified-work requirements need checking before alteration. Ordinary mesh-screen work does not establish that a security door can be modified.

Lifting floor edges and doorway trims

Minor laminate and vinyl repairs can address suitable damaged boards, planks and doorway transition strips. Click-lock and glued floors need different access; matching material and surrounding flooring affect a local replacement. A lifting bedroom doorway trim can be a trip hazard, while a swollen board near a laundry needs the moisture source investigated before reinstatement.

Lifting edges

Moisture, restricted movement, damaged joints or an uneven substrate can lift laminate edges. The visible edge does not identify the cause by itself. The floor system and affected area need assessment before a board or trim is replaced.

Moisture before replacement

Continuing moisture should be investigated before replacement floorboards are installed. The source, substrate condition and suitability for reinstatement matter. Replacing a swollen board without resolving dampness can expose the new material to the same damage.

Why is laminate lifting at its edges?

Moisture, movement restriction, joint damage or an uneven backing can contribute. Ellis checks the system and fault history before choosing a local repair. A new trim or heavier fixing will not necessarily resolve the underlying problem.

Should moisture damage be addressed before floorboard replacement?

Yes. Ellis assesses the moisture source and substrate condition before reinstatement is agreed. Drying, other trade work or backing repairs may be needed. Replacing the visible board alone does not establish that the cause is resolved.

Choose cleaning to suit the paving and its joints

Accessible patio and paving cleaning needs a method suited to the surface, joints and coating. Slippery courtyard paving or driveway stains before an inspection need the stain and runoff considered. A lower-impact method may suit fragile finishes; cleaning does not guarantee stain removal or an inspection outcome.

Note flaking coatings, loose pavers, nearby doors and runoff paths. Confirm water availability, access and protection or cleanup requirements. Loose or sunken paving needs its own repair assessment.

Damage from unsuitable cleaning

Unsuitable pressure can dislodge weak jointing, damage coatings or erode vulnerable surfaces. The condition and material determine the method. Cleaning needs protection and drainage planned; it cannot correct loose pavers, a failed base or recurring pooling.

Suspect older material

Suspected asbestos-containing material must not be pressure cleaned. Keep unidentified sheets and finishes undisturbed. We check the patio and paving materials before choosing a cleaning method; roof and asbestos cleaning are not included in this service.

Can pressure cleaning damage pavers or grout?

Yes. Weak jointing and vulnerable finishes can be damaged by an unsuitable method. Ellis assesses the surface, coatings, joints and drainage first. Paving repairs may need a separate scope before cleaning is suitable.

Repairs for older residents and support-rail enquiries

Bring any placement plan from your adviser and identify tiled areas or concealed services. The quote identifies supply responsibility, the rail, fixings and any support or finish work. Installation alone is not an accessibility assessment or guarantee.

Maintenance and funded modifications

We repair worn or damaged fittings and assess home modifications against your installation needs. If funding is involved, confirm the approval and payment arrangements before booking so we can agree the work and quote with you.

Placement and structural fixing

A grab rail’s position must suit its intended user and the appropriate placement plan, while its fixings need suitable structural support. Tiles or plasterboard alone do not establish support. Ellis checks product, location and wall conditions before agreeing installation.

Do not rely on towel fittings

A bathroom towel rail is not a substitute for an assessed support rail. Its product and fixings are not established for a person’s balance or transfer load. A suitable grab rail needs its own placement, product and structural support assessment.

Pergola supports and movement

A sagging beam, moving post, deteriorated connection or altered roof load can require structural advice. Cosmetic trim repairs do not resolve support failure. Ellis identifies the appropriate qualified assessment before disturbing or loading affected components.

Unidentified older eaves sheets

Leave unidentified eaves sheets undisturbed. Tell us where the damage is; we arrange material assessment and confirm the repair plan before cutting, drilling or removing lining.

What fits a small-repair visit

A handyman visit can address defined minor repairs and installations around the home. The task, material and condition control the scope; an electrical, plumbing, structural or safety-critical fault needs the appropriate Ellis team.

When a trade assessment comes first

Fixed wiring, water-service work, gas faults and structural concerns cannot be accepted merely as odd jobs. Ellis scopes these separately with its own appropriately licensed personnel; naming the symptom helps identify the right assessment.

Strata Maintenance & Authorised Access

For strata maintenance, identify the exact lot or common-property location and the person authorised to approve assessment and repairs. An occupant's request to attend does not establish approval for work on common property. Bring the approved task list, spending limit if applicable and the contact for any variation.

Ellis agrees tenant or occupant appointments, keys, parking and shared-area access before attendance. Flag noisy work and any building access rules. Repair findings and extra work go back to the nominated approval contact before proceeding. Use our property maintenance service for the wider repair list and managed-property coordination guide to prepare the authority and access details.

Vacate repairs and handover

Vacate repairs address the authorised repair list: for example loose cupboard hardware, damaged wall patches or sticking doors. Bring the property manager's marked list if available and distinguish damage to repair from cleaning tasks. End-of-lease cleaning has its own agreed checklist and quote.

Confirm the approval contact, key collection, access window and planned handover date. Ellis scopes the repairs, materials and desired finish, seeks approval for variations and agrees how completion details will be handed over. Existing photos are optional; do not remove fittings to prepare them. A repair booking does not guarantee a bond refund or an inspection result. See wall patching and finish choices where a vacate list includes plaster damage.

Requested dates and urgent hazards

Hazards before routine bookings

Smoke, sparks, gas odour, rapidly spreading water, collapse risk or a person in immediate danger should not wait for a routine handyman appointment. Keep clear of the hazard and use the appropriate emergency response; do not dismantle equipment to investigate.

Correct emergency personnel

An urgent handyman request does not replace an emergency electrician, plumber, gas specialist or emergency services. The hazard determines the response. Ellis’s regulated work uses its own appropriately licensed personnel, with availability and the accepted appointment confirmed directly.

Handyman Booking Questions

Can I book several small repairs in one visit?

Yes. Tell Ellis your suburb and the jobs you want completed. We assess them together and agree the repair list, materials and quote. Grouping compatible tasks lets you organise cupboard repairs, assembly and other small jobs through one contact.

How are handyman jobs quoted?

We provide a written quote after an on-site assessment. Ask whether the job is priced by time or as a booking block, and which labour, materials and disposal costs are included. Additional repairs need their scope and price agreed before proceeding.

Can I supply the furniture, fittings or replacement parts?

Yes. Share the product details and tell us what is already on site. We check compatibility, missing hardware and installation requirements before confirming the work. Product supply and any extra materials are identified in the quote.

Can you work at rentals, strata properties or business premises?

Yes. Confirm the person authorised to approve repairs and arrange entry. Tell us about tenant appointments, common-property approval, parking and trading hours so we can agree access before the visit.

Do I need photos or measurements for a handyman enquiry?

No. Describe the problem by phone or through Contact Us and provide your suburb. Photos, measurements and a repair list are optional. Our Perth team checks the property on site and helps define the work.

What happens if my repair needs a specialist trade?

Ellis identifies electrical, plumbing, gas, structural and other specialist requirements during assessment. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. Fixed wiring, wet-area waterproofing and structural changes are separate from ordinary small repairs.

Maintenance and repair questions

What needs checking before a letterbox post is installed?

Ellis checks the proposed position, boundaries, access, ground conditions, underground services and supplied product's support requirements before digging. Tell us about known service locations and whether an old post must be removed. The assessment and quote identify the agreed installation work rather than relying on appearance.

Can you mount a garden hose reel on a brick wall?

Yes. Provide the reel model and proposed position. We check the masonry, fixing points, clearance and supplied brackets before fitting. Crumbling brickwork or damaged mortar needs assessment rather than simply using a larger screw.

Who can assess minor repairs to a Perth pergola?

Ellis can assess local pergola repairs, separating trim and lining work from damaged supports or structural movement. Describe loose timber, sagging components or recurring water damage. The assessment establishes the material and suitable work before any repair is approved.

Can you assess damaged eaves sheets on an older house?

Yes. Tell us the building age and visible damage without disturbing the sheets. The material needs identification before drilling, sanding or removal, because older linings may contain asbestos. We confirm the safe assessment and work required rather than assume a simple patch.

Can a handyman clean a small patio in Perth?

Ellis can assess cleaning for a small patio, choosing a method suited to the paving, joints, coatings and drainage. Describe the surface and stains. Fragile materials or suspected hazardous materials require assessment before pressure cleaning is considered.

Can paving cleaning be combined with a rental courtyard tidy-up?

Discuss both tasks with Ellis and identify the paving, garden waste and small repairs involved. We confirm what can be grouped and which work needs a separate service. Waste removal and any disposal charges should be included explicitly in the quote.

Can you replace a damaged laminate board or vinyl plank?

Yes, where the flooring system and available replacement material allow a local repair. Click-lock boards and glued flooring need different access and preparation. Ellis checks how much surrounding flooring must be lifted and whether spare matching material is available before quoting.
