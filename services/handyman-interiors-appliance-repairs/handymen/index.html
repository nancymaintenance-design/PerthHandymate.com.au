Handyman Booking Questions

Can I book several small repairs in one visit?

Yes. Tell Ellis your suburb and the jobs you want completed. We assess them together and agree the repair list, materials and quote. Grouping compatible tasks lets you organise cupboard repairs, assembly and other small jobs through one contact.

How are handyman jobs quoted?

We provide a written quote after an on-site assessment. Ask whether the job is priced by time or as a booking block, and which labour, materials and disposal costs are included. Additional repairs need their scope and price agreed before proceeding.

Can I supply the furniture, fittings or replacement parts?

Yes. Share the product details and tell us what is already on site. We check compatibility, missing hardware and installation requirements before confirming the work. Product supply and any extra materials are identified in the quote.

Can you work at rentals, strata properties or business premises?

Yes. Confirm the person authorised to approve repairs and arrange entry. Tell us about tenant appointments, common-property approval, parking and trading hours so we can agree access before the visit.

Do I need photos or measurements before contacting Ellis?

No. Describe the problem by phone or through Contact Us and provide your suburb. Photos, measurements and a repair list are optional. Our Perth team checks the property on site and helps define the work.

What happens if my repair needs a specialist trade?

Ellis identifies electrical, plumbing, gas, structural and other specialist requirements during assessment. We explain the work and confirm the qualified arrangements before it proceeds. Fixed wiring, wet-area waterproofing and structural changes are separate from ordinary small repairs.
