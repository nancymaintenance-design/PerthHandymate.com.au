Pet Door Installation: Dogs & Cats

Pet door installation uses one assessment for dog doors and cat flaps, with the opening suited to the pet and the door construction. Tell Ellis the pet size, product model and whether the panel is timber, ordinary insect-screen mesh or glass. For a suitable timber door we check thickness, framing and the proposed cut-out; on mesh screens we check whether the frame can support the product.

Can a pet door be fitted directly into glass?

The glazing must be assessed before a fitting method is agreed. Do not cut or drill the existing pane yourself; a purpose-made replacement pane may be needed. Glazing work and any security-screen changes have separate product and qualified-work requirements. A pet flap must not be assumed to preserve a screen's security performance.

Existing photos are optional. Ellis checks the opening on site and confirms the product, fitting method, supply and disposal scope before quoting.

Pressure Cleaning for Patios & Paving

Our handyman pressure-cleaning scope is suitable accessible patios and paving. Ellis checks the surface, coating, joints and drainage before selecting a cleaning method; more pressure is not automatically appropriate for a fragile finish. Tell us about flaking coatings, loose pavers, nearby doors and where runoff can go.

Does this include roof or asbestos cleaning?

No. Roof cleaning is outside this patio and paving task, and suspected asbestos is excluded from pressure cleaning. Leave suspect material undisturbed and ask for the appropriate assessment. Surface cleaning also does not repair a failed base or persistent pooling: use paving repair assessment for loose or sunken areas.

Confirm the area to be cleaned, access, water availability and protection or cleanup requirements when Ellis prepares the written quote.

Letterboxes, posts and hose-reel fixings

Before ground disturbance

A proposed letterbox post position needs checks for underground services, boundaries, nearby access and the installation requirements. Do not assume an empty-looking patch of ground is clear. Ellis agrees the location and necessary checks before digging.

Weather and outdoor fixings

Weather exposure, corrosion, movement and deteriorating wall material can loosen outdoor fixings. A replacement screw cannot restore damaged support by itself. The fitting, fixing points and surrounding material need checking together before further loading.

Toughened glass is not a site cut-out

An existing toughened-glass pane cannot be treated as an ordinary on-site cut-out. Holes are prepared before toughening; see the manufacturer’s toughened-glass guidance. A glazier assesses whether a purpose-made replacement pane and compatible pet-door product are required.

Preserving security requirements

A pet-door opening can affect a security door’s complete product system and performance. Product approval, installation requirements and relevant qualified-work requirements need checking before alteration. Ordinary mesh-screen work does not establish that a security door can be modified.

Lifting floor edges and doorway trims

Lifting edges

Moisture, restricted movement, damaged joints or an uneven substrate can lift laminate edges. The visible edge does not identify the cause by itself. The floor system and affected area need assessment before a board or trim is replaced.

Moisture before replacement

Continuing moisture should be investigated before replacement floorboards are installed. The source, substrate condition and suitability for reinstatement matter. Replacing a swollen board without resolving dampness can expose the new material to the same damage.

Read more about lifting floor edges and doorway trims for related preparation and questions.

Patio trim, supports and unidentified eaves lining

Pergola supports and movement

A sagging beam, moving post, deteriorated connection or altered roof load can require structural advice. Cosmetic trim repairs do not resolve support failure. Ellis identifies the appropriate qualified assessment before disturbing or loading affected components.

Unidentified older eaves sheets

Older eaves lining can contain asbestos, which cannot be ruled out by a photograph alone. Leave unidentified sheets undisturbed until assessed. See WorkSafe asbestos guidance. Material identification and the work plan come before cutting, drilling or removing lining.

Choose cleaning to suit the paving and its joints

Damage from unsuitable cleaning

Unsuitable pressure can dislodge weak jointing, damage coatings or erode vulnerable surfaces. The condition and material determine the method. Cleaning needs protection and drainage planned; it cannot correct loose pavers, a failed base or recurring pooling.

Suspect older material

Suspected asbestos-containing material must not be pressure cleaned. Older unidentified sheets and finishes require assessment before disturbance; appearance alone cannot establish safety. See WorkSafe asbestos guidance. The patio and paving task does not include roof or asbestos cleaning.

Repairs for older residents and support-rail enquiries

Maintenance and funded modifications

Ordinary maintenance repairs worn or damaged items; disability-related home modifications respond to individual needs and funding decisions. They are not automatically the same service. Read NDIS home-modification guidance and confirm the individual funding and payment arrangements separately before booking.

Placement and structural fixing

A grab rail’s position must suit its intended user and the appropriate placement plan, while its fixings need suitable structural support. Tiles or plasterboard alone do not establish support. Ellis checks product, location and wall conditions before agreeing installation.

Do not rely on towel fittings

A bathroom towel rail is not a substitute for an assessed support rail. Its product and fixings are not established for a person’s balance or transfer load. A suitable grab rail needs its own placement, product and structural support assessment.

Handyman Booking Questions

Choosing the right help for small repairs

What fits a small-repair visit

A handyman visit can address defined minor repairs and installations around the home. The task, material and condition control the scope; an electrical, plumbing, structural or safety-critical fault needs the appropriate Ellis team.

When a trade assessment comes first

Fixed wiring, water-service work, gas faults and structural concerns cannot be accepted merely as odd jobs. Ellis scopes these separately with its own appropriately licensed personnel; naming the symptom helps identify the right assessment.

Read more about choosing the right help for small repairs for related preparation and questions.

Grouping jobs without losing the detail

Shared setup and realistic savings

Combining suitable tasks may avoid repeated travel or preparation, but different tools, missing parts and return stages can change the work. Compare the full written scope rather than assume a bundle will always cost less.

A useful repair list

For each repair, note the room, faulty item, symptom, quantity and preferred outcome. Add your suburb, priorities, access limits, approvals and any supplied products. Photos and measurements are optional; Ellis can assess the details on site.

Read more about grouping jobs without losing the detail for related preparation and questions.

Requested deadlines and immediate hazards

Hazards before routine bookings

Smoke, sparks, gas odour, rapidly spreading water, collapse risk or a person in immediate danger should not wait for a routine handyman appointment. Keep clear of the hazard and use the appropriate emergency response; do not dismantle equipment to investigate.

Correct emergency personnel

An urgent handyman request does not replace an emergency electrician, plumber, gas specialist or emergency services. The hazard determines the response. Ellis’s regulated work uses its own appropriately licensed personnel, with availability and the accepted appointment confirmed directly.

Read more about requested deadlines and immediate hazards for related preparation and questions.

Compare the full quote and verify the relevant trade

Written quote inclusions

A useful quote identifies the tasks, labour basis, materials, supply responsibility, access assumptions, removal, disposal and exclusions. It should explain how changes are approved. Published service ranges do not establish the total for a specific property before assessment.

Check identity and scope-specific qualifications

Check the business name, contact details and the personnel responsible for the proposed work. Relevant qualifications are specific to the task; WA electrical-work guidance and WA security licence classes explain different work categories. Ask for applicable details rather than treating a handyman business name as a universal licence.

Can I book several small repairs in one visit?

Yes. Tell Ellis your suburb and the jobs you want completed. We assess them together and agree the repair list, materials and quote. Grouping compatible tasks lets you organise cupboard repairs, assembly and other small jobs through one contact.

How are handyman jobs quoted?

We provide a written quote after an on-site assessment. Ask whether the job is priced by time or as a booking block, and which labour, materials and disposal costs are included. Additional repairs need their scope and price agreed before proceeding.

Can I supply the furniture, fittings or replacement parts?

Yes. Share the product details and tell us what is already on site. We check compatibility, missing hardware and installation requirements before confirming the work. Product supply and any extra materials are identified in the quote.

Can you work at rentals, strata properties or business premises?

Yes. Confirm the person authorised to approve repairs and arrange entry. Tell us about tenant appointments, common-property approval, parking and trading hours so we can agree access before the visit.

Do I need photos or measurements before contacting Ellis?

No. Describe the problem by phone or through Contact Us and provide your suburb. Photos, measurements and a repair list are optional. Our Perth team checks the property on site and helps define the work.

What happens if my repair needs a specialist trade?

Ellis identifies electrical, plumbing, gas, structural and other specialist requirements during assessment. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. Fixed wiring, wet-area waterproofing and structural changes are separate from ordinary small repairs.
