Rangehood installation, repairs and cleaning

Installation, a performance fault and grease cleaning need different checks. Describe the work you want and what is already in place so we can assess the rangehood and confirm the scope.

Rangehood installation and replacement

We check the supplied rangehood, intended cabinet or mounting position and available access before agreeing installation. Tell us whether an existing unit needs removal and what is to be retained.

Mounting, cabinet adjustments, ducting and electrical changes are identified separately when defining the work. We confirm what is included before work starts.

Rangehood repairs

If the rangehood will not start, makes unusual noise, has weak extraction or has a light or control fault, describe what happens and share the model if known. Symptoms alone do not identify the failed part.

We assess the appliance condition, access and compatible parts before confirming whether a repair is suitable. Diagnosis, parts and any removal or connection work are identified in the quote.

Rangehood cleaning

For rangehood cleaning, tell us about visible grease on the filters and accessible surfaces, the model and any access restrictions. The suitable method depends on the manufacturer's instructions, filter type and appliance condition.

Filter cleaning, filter replacement and internal cleaning are different tasks. We confirm the cleaning area and any separate work before quoting. Existing photos are optional; do not dismantle the appliance to prepare an enquiry.

Rangehood FAQs

Can you install a rangehood I have purchased?

Yes. Share the model and intended position, and tell us whether an old unit needs removal. We check the product, mounting space and access before confirming the installation scope and written quote.

Do you offer rangehood repairs and cleaning?

Yes. Describe the fault or the cleaning required. We check the model, condition, access and any parts or filter requirements before agreeing which work is suitable.

Are ducting and electrical work included?

These are separate scope items. Tell us about the existing ducting and power connection. We confirm what is included in the quote; any agreed regulated electrical work is completed by our own appropriately licensed employees.

Do I need to send photos for a quote?

No. Your suburb, the rangehood model if known and a description of the work are enough to start. Existing photos are optional. Do not dismantle the appliance to prepare an enquiry.
