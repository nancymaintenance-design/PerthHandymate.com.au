Minor carpentry and timber repairs

Damaged trim after flooring or a furniture move

Loose skirting, chipped architraves and damaged door trim can often be assessed as a grouped repair. Ellis checks the profile and surrounding surface so replacement sections fit the agreed finish rather than stand out unnecessarily.

Local timber repair or a larger replacement?

Discuss timber repairs

What we check on site

Matching replacement skirting boards and architraves

Local skirting board and architrave repairs can replace a damaged section or secure suitable loose trim. Matching profile, thickness, joints and the surrounding floor finish affects how a new section fits the room.

Old-profile availability and sound fixing material determine whether a local match is practical. Confirm painting or staining separately from fitting the timber; new trim does not automatically arrive with the same finish as the existing room.

Dog-chewed skirting and gaps after a flooring change need different checks. Ellis assesses the damaged length, floor clearance and remaining profile before agreeing replacement. Swelling near the floor calls for moisture investigation rather than a cosmetic strip over the damage.

Swelling near floor level

Skirting can swell where moisture reaches timber or moisture-sensitive board. Wet cleaning, leaks or damp adjacent construction may be involved. Replacing the visible trim before the cause is understood can leave the next section exposed to the same damage.

Paint after trim replacement

Replacement skirting may need filling, preparation and painting or staining to suit the agreed finish. Profile matching and colour matching are separate questions. The quote identifies the finish work rather than assuming installation includes repainting the whole room.

Soft or rotten outdoor timber

Minor timber repair can address local damage to trim or suitable frame sections where sound material remains. External weathered trim and rotten timber at a door-frame base require different checks from replacing a decorative indoor strip.

The extent of decay, moisture source, matching timber and whether the member carries load determine local repair versus replacement. Ellis identifies support or structural concerns before agreeing carpentry; a patch cannot restore an unassessed structural member.

For soft timber at the bottom of a door frame or loose weathered trim under a patio, describe the location and earlier repairs. Leave unstable parts undisturbed and mention suspected pest activity so the assessment can address both the damage and its cause.

Decay and pest damage

Soft, crumbling or hollow timber can have different causes, including moisture decay or pest activity. Appearance alone cannot confirm which is present. Appropriate timber and pest assessment helps identify the cause before new timber conceals the affected area.

Load-bearing timber

Decay affecting posts, beams, supports or connections needs its structural significance checked. The extent may be hidden beyond the visible soft section. Keep clear of unstable areas; qualified assessment determines what can be retained and what needs a wider repair.

Carpenters service guide

Carpenters: Common Questions

Service details

  • Repair or replace damaged timber trim, doors, frames and skirting
  • Build shelving, storage or other fitted timber elements
  • Complete scoped framing, lining or finishing carpentry
Do you carry out small timber repairs as well as larger carpentry jobs?

Yes. Tell us about damaged skirting, architraves, doors, trim or fitted storage. We check the affected timber and agree whether a local repair, replacement section or a larger job is appropriate. You can include several small repairs in one assessment.

Can you repair or replace damaged skirting boards and architraves?

A damaged section may be replaced while retaining the rest if the profile, material and available matching timber are suitable. We check the join, surrounding condition and intended paint or finish. Tell Ellis whether you need local repair or new trim, and mention moisture or soft timber so the cause is assessed before replacement.

Can you adjust a timber door that rubs against its frame?

We check the door, hinges, frame and point where it catches before recommending an adjustment or repair. Tell us if it also has loose fittings or damaged timber. New door installation is a separate task, so we distinguish an adjustment from supplying and fitting a replacement.

What happens if you find rotten or repeatedly damaged timber?

The extent and cause need checking before a local repair is chosen. Tell Ellis if timber gets wet, paint repeatedly fails or the area has been repaired before. Load-bearing timber or significant movement may need structural assessment before carpentry begins. Suspected asbestos must be assessed before disturbance.

Can you help with timber repairs before moving house or a rental inspection?

We can assess trim, doors and fitted-storage repairs as a list of jobs. For a rental property, confirm who authorises the repairs and access. We agree the work and required finish with you; we do not guarantee an inspection or sale result.

What affects the carpentry quote, and must I send measurements?

Timber condition, matching materials, fittings, access and the finish affect the quote. We measure and assess on site before providing a written quote. You can start with a description and your suburb; existing photos or dimensions are optional.

Why are skirting boards swelling near the floor?

Moisture can affect timber and moisture-sensitive board, but the source needs assessment. Tell Ellis about leaks or repeated dampness. New skirting should be considered alongside the cause rather than simply covering the swollen section.

Does replacement skirting need painting?

It may need preparation and painting or staining, depending on the product and desired finish. Ellis confirms whether that work is included. Matching the old profile does not automatically establish an exact paint match.

Can I tell rot from termite damage by looking at it?

Appearance alone is insufficient. Tell Ellis about softness, hollow areas, moisture and any pest evidence without disturbing the timber. Timber condition and possible pest activity need the appropriate assessment before repair is chosen.

When does rotten timber need structural assessment?

When supports, beams, posts, connections or visible movement are involved, a local patch is not enough to define the work. Ellis identifies the appropriate assessment and repair scope before disturbing or replacing affected members.

Can you repair skirting gaps left after new flooring?

Yes. Tell us where gaps remain and whether the skirting was removed or damaged during flooring work. We assess the existing profile, floor level and required finish before agreeing repair or replacement. Painting and any additional flooring work should be identified in the quote.

Site assessment and enquiry details

Tell Ellis what needs repairing or building and the finish you want. We inspect existing timber or measure for new work on site and confirm the carpentry scope and quote.
