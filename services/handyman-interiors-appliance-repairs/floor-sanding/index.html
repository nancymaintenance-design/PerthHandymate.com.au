Floor Sanding service guide

Floor Sanding in Perth: Common Questions

Can worn timber floors be sanded and recoated?

Suitable timber floors can be sanded and prepared for a compatible coating. We first assess the timber, existing finish and board condition. The agreed scope includes the accessible floor area, edges and your preferred colour and sheen.

Should loose boards or moisture damage be checked before floor sanding?

Yes. Board movement, moisture damage and protruding fixings need assessment before sanding starts. Surface finishing does not resolve an underlying moisture or support problem. Tell us about known leaks or movement when arranging the assessment.

What preparation is needed in an occupied home?

Tell us which rooms are occupied and what furniture needs moving. Access, dust isolation and ventilation affect the work plan. Existing coatings or unknown floor materials may need additional controls, which are confirmed during assessment.

Floor Sanding Work We Assess

  • Assess timber condition, coating build-up and board movement
  • Sand accessible timber floors and edges to an agreed level
  • Apply a compatible coating system after colour and sheen selection

Floor Sanding: On-site Checks & Optional Details

  • Room dimensions and clear photos in natural light
  • Timber type and existing coating if known
