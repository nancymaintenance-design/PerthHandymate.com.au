What we can coordinate

A practical route for floor sanding

Floor sanding can restore suitable timber floors by removing worn coatings, smoothing boards and preparing the surface for a chosen finish.

Common requests

  • Assess timber condition, coating build-up and board movement
  • Sand accessible timber floors and edges to an agreed level
  • Apply a compatible coating system after colour and sheen selection

Safety and qualifications

Old coatings, dust and unknown floor materials can require special controls; occupied areas need isolation and adequate ventilation during the work.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
