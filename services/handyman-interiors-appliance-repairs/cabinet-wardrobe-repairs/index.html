Cabinet & Wardrobe Repairs in Perth

Loose cupboard doors, drawers that bind and wardrobe panels that leave their guides need different checks. Ellis assesses the existing cabinet or wardrobe and its fittings before recommending adjustment, compatible hardware or a separate replacement scope.

Tell us which door drops, rubs or pulls away from its mounting plate. The hinge, plate, screws and material around the fixing holes all matter. Tightening a screw repeatedly may not help when the fixing hole or surrounding board is damaged. Ellis checks alignment and the fixing condition before agreeing the repair.

Drawer runner replacement and adjustment

A drawer that tilts, jams or comes off its travel may have a worn runner, damaged fixing or another alignment problem. Share which drawer is affected and whether it catches when empty or loaded. Runner length, side clearance, mounting position and the drawer condition affect compatibility; soft-close and ordinary runners are not automatically interchangeable.

Hinged and sliding wardrobe doors

Wardrobe door repairs start with the panel type. Hinged panels need hinge and clearance checks; sliding panels need guides, stops, tracks and rollers considered together. Mirrored panels also need their condition and safe handling assessed. Do not force or lift out a loose mirrored door to prepare for a visit.

Assessment details for cabinet & wardrobe repairs

A dropped cupboard door needs more than tightening

Cupboard door and cabinet hinge repairs address dropped doors, alignment and damaged mounting points. The hinge and the cabinet board form one fixing system; a replacement hinge needs sound material around its fixings.

A broken hinge may be replaceable with compatible hardware. Stripped holes or swollen board can require a different repair or a replacement panel. Ellis checks both before recommending further hardware purchases or a soft-close change.

A kitchen door fallen from its hinge or screws stripped from particleboard needs the loose door supported safely and the damaged area assessed. Do not keep tightening a failing screw; record the affected fittings and avoid forcing the door.

Repeatedly loose screws

Cupboard screws may loosen because fixing holes are worn, the mounting plate moves or the door is misaligned. Damaged particleboard may no longer hold the screw. The surrounding material needs checking before the same screw is tightened again.

Hardware or cabinet material

Replacing a worn hinge can help only if its mounting points remain suitable. Crumbling, split or moisture-swollen board changes that decision. Ellis separates hinge replacement, fixing repair and panel work in the scope after assessing the cabinet.

Drawers and wardrobe panels that leave their guides

Drawer repairs and runner replacement address binding, tilting and a drawer coming off its travel. Sliding wardrobe doors need their guides, rollers, stops and tracks assessed for the actual panel system.

Soft-close replacement and older cabinets require runner length, mounting position and clearance checks. Compatible fittings may be available without replacing the cabinet, but an upgrade is not universal. Mirrored wardrobe panels require safe handling and separate condition checks.

A kitchen drawer that will not close when loaded may have a runner or alignment fault. A wardrobe door scraping its bottom track can indicate guide, roller or support problems. Mention whether the symptom occurs empty or loaded without removing an unstable panel.

Recurring drawer derailment

A drawer may leave its runners when guides are worn, fixings move, alignment is wrong or the drawer itself is damaged. Load can expose an existing weakness. Refitting the drawer alone will not repair an incompatible or failing runner system.

Replacement runners in older cabinetry

An older cabinet can accept new runners only where the dimensions, side clearance and mounting surfaces suit the replacement. Soft-close systems may require different clearances. Matching compatibility is more useful than choosing parts by appearance alone.

Cabinet & Wardrobe Repairs questions

Can stripped cupboard hinge holes be repaired?

Ellis checks the hinge, mounting plate, fixing holes and surrounding cabinet material first. A local repair may suit sound cabinetry, while swollen or extensively damaged board can change the options. The repair method and any replacement fittings are agreed after assessment.

Can you replace a drawer runner with soft-close hardware?

Runner length, mounting positions, side clearance and drawer condition must suit the proposed hardware. Soft-close parts are not a universal replacement. Share the product details if available; Ellis confirms compatibility, parts and any extra work before quoting an upgrade.

Do sliding wardrobe doors use the same parts as patio sliders?

No. Wardrobe guides, stops, tracks and rollers need checks for that panel system. Mirrored panels also require condition and handling assessment. Do not force an unstable panel or buy replacement rollers until the system and compatible parts are identified.

Will a repair include new cabinet panels or disposal?

New panels, replacement cabinetry, finish matching and disposal are separate scope decisions. Tell Ellis what you want assessed and who can approve the work. The written quote states the agreed repair, supplied parts, removal and disposal rather than assuming these are included.

Detailed cabinet & wardrobe repairs questions

Why do cupboard hinge screws keep coming loose?

Worn holes, a moving mounting plate or misalignment can repeatedly load the fixings. Damaged board may not hold the screw. Ellis checks the hinge and mounting material together so the repair addresses the cause.

Should I replace the hinge or repair the cabinet panel?

The hinge condition and the strength of its mounting points decide this. A sound panel may suit compatible replacement hardware; damaged board needs its own repair assessment. Ellis explains the options before the work is agreed.

Why does a drawer keep coming off its runners?

Worn guides, loose fixings, alignment or drawer damage can cause recurring derailment. Tell Ellis whether it happens when empty or loaded. The drawer and both runners need checking rather than simply putting it back on its tracks.

Can new drawer runners fit an older cabinet?

Sometimes. Ellis checks runner length, clearance, mounting positions and cabinet condition against the proposed replacement. Soft-close and ordinary runners are not automatically interchangeable; any modification or upgrade needs separate agreement.
