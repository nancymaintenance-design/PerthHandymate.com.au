Flat Pack Furniture Assembly: Beds, Desks & Storage

A small desk, a bed frame and a tall storage unit can require different space, lifting and fixing arrangements. Share the product name or model and quantity if known. We check the manufacturer's assembly instructions and identify any separate wall attachment or extra materials before work starts.

Furniture Assembly: Delivery, Parts & Instructions

Make the furniture and room accessible

Confirm whether the packs are already in the room where the furniture will stand, and tell us about stairs, lifts and parking. Agree any unpacking, movement of packs or existing furniture before the visit. Leave enough room to lay out the parts and assemble the item without blocking access.

Check instructions and hardware

Keep the instructions, fittings and delivered packs together. If parts are damaged or missing, the assembly may need to pause while compatible replacements are obtained. Tell us about an opened pack or partly assembled item so we can allow for checking it. We do not assume substitute fixings will suit a product.

Wall anchoring and the finished position

Some furniture requires attachment to a suitable supporting wall under its product instructions. Ellis checks the proposed position, wall material, support and supplied anchoring hardware before confirming the method. For rentals or shared properties, confirm permission for wall fixings with the authorised contact.

After assembly we check the furniture's operation, fittings and agreed position. Any unresolved parts or fixing requirement is explained. Wall anchoring is not an unqualified promise for every wall or product; the supporting surface and instructions determine the suitable approach.

Why tall furniture needs anchoring checks

Some tall furniture needs manufacturer-specified anchoring to reduce the risk of tipping. The supplied bracket must suit both the product and supporting wall. Assembly does not establish that the wall lining or generic supplied fixings provide suitable support.

Furniture Assembly questions

Can I supply the flat-pack furniture?

Yes. Tell Ellis the product, quantity and whether the packs and instructions are already on site. We check the supplied parts and assembly requirements, then confirm labour and any extra materials in the written quote.

What happens if a part is missing or damaged?

Assembly may need to pause while the correct replacement is obtained. Tell us about known missing parts or damage before the visit. Ellis explains what remains incomplete rather than assuming an unsuitable substitute will work.

Can you anchor tall furniture to the wall?

We assess the product instructions, proposed position, supporting wall and compatible fixings. Confirm permission for wall attachments where needed. The quote identifies the anchoring work that can be included.

Do you remove the packaging?

Tell us whether you want packaging left in an agreed place or removed. Handling and disposal are separate scope items and are included only when agreed in the quote.
