Carpet Repair — Work & Quote Details

A burn, tear or damaged patch in one room

Ellis checks the damaged area, carpet construction and any matching offcut you have. A patch needs a suitable piece and a stable surrounding carpet. We explain the likely appearance before you approve the work.

Loose edges and wrinkles near a doorway

A lifting join or wrinkle may involve the fixing, underlay or carpet tension. We assess these together rather than hide the edge with a trim. Wider wear or unavailable matching carpet may make replacement worth comparing.

Carpet Repair service guide

Carpet Repair in Perth: Common Questions

Can a small burnt or damaged carpet patch be repaired?

Sometimes, when a compatible donor piece is available and the surrounding carpet is sound. Colour, pile direction and wear affect how the patch will look. Tell Ellis whether you have offcuts; if a suitable patch is unavailable, compare replacement carpet for the room before choosing the scope.

Can loose carpet at a bedroom doorway be repaired?

Loose edges, joins and doorway transitions can be assessed for repair. We check the carpet tension and surrounding fixings before confirming the work. If the carpet is damp or there is a history of leaks, the moisture issue needs assessment first.

Carpet Repair Work We Assess

  • Re-stretch loose or rippled carpet
  • Patch damaged sections using a compatible donor piece
  • Repair joins, edges, transitions or lifting around doorways

Carpet Repair: On-site Checks & Optional Details
