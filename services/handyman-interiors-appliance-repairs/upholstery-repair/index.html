What we can coordinate

A practical route for upholstery repair

Upholstery repair restores suitable furniture through fabric, padding, webbing, stitching or local frame-related work after condition review.

Common requests

  • Repair torn seams, worn panels or localised fabric damage
  • Replace padding, webbing or support materials in suitable furniture
  • Recover a chair, cushion or selected furniture piece in a chosen material

Safety and qualifications

Mould, pest contamination, broken recliner mechanisms or unstable frames may need separate treatment before upholstery work.

Any licence, qualification, approval or specialist requirement is checked against the actual work and location before it proceeds.
