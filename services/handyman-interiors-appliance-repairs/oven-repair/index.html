Oven Repair service guide

Oven Repair in Perth: Common Questions

Can an oven that no longer heats be repaired?

No-heat and uneven-heating faults can be assessed for repair. Tell us the model, fuel type and which functions are affected. The fault, appliance condition and parts availability determine whether component replacement is viable.

Can damaged oven door hinges or seals be replaced?

Serviceable hinges and seals may be replaced when compatible parts are available. The door and surrounding components need checking first. Provide the appliance model and describe whether the door drops, fails to close or lets heat escape.

What should I do if the oven sparks, overheats or smells of gas?

Stop using it. Do not dismantle the appliance or test it again. A suspected gas leak needs an urgent safety response: avoid flames and electrical switching, leave the area and contact the gas emergency service; use emergency services for immediate danger. For non-emergency gas appliance work or hard-wired electrical repair, Ellis uses its own appropriately licensed personnel.
