Plasterers in Perth: Common Questions

What work is typically included in Plasterers?

Plastering covers wall and ceiling repairs, new linings, joints, cornices and surfaces that need preparation before decorating.

  • Patch holes, impact damage and localised ceiling defects
  • Install plasterboard and finish joints for scoped alterations
  • Repair or replace cornice and prepare surfaces for painting
What information helps confirm the scope?

Clear property information helps identify the right approach and site-specific constraints before work is arranged.

  • Photos with an object for scale and the affected dimensions
  • Whether damage is on a wall, ceiling, wet area or external surface
  • Any leak, movement, cracking or previous repair history
Are there safety, licensing or suitability issues to consider?

Active leaks, structural movement and suspect asbestos-containing materials must be resolved or assessed before plaster is disturbed.

Requirements depend on the actual task, property and location, so they are checked before work proceeds.
