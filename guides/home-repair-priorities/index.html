Repairs before an Open Home: Perth Property Priorities

Updated 2026-10-14

An open home or property photography date is approaching, and the list includes a sticking door, damaged skirting and marked walls. The most visible item is not always the one to address first. A wall mark may be cosmetic, while swelling or recurring stains can point to a cause that needs assessment before any finish is applied.

How should cosmetic repairs be separated from underlying defects, and which household faults should not wait for an ordinary appointment? This guide explains a practical priority order, how to record dependencies and when to split a presentation deadline from work needed for safety, security or continuing damage.

Use consequences to sort the list

Describe what each fault prevents or puts at risk. A door that will not secure, an unstable handrail and a paint scuff do not have the same consequences. Note whether damage is active, whether access is affected and whether the condition is changing. Immediate hazards require the appropriate response rather than a place in an ordinary handyman queue.

Next, identify work that depends on another problem being resolved. Painting a recurring damp mark before investigating moisture can conceal a symptom without addressing its cause. Replacing swollen skirting has a similar dependency. Keep the original observation in the brief even when the visible repair is small, so presentation work does not quietly become the whole scope.

Plan the deadline without promising the outcome

Tell Ellis the open-home or photography date as well as the repair list, then ask what can be assessed and completed in the available arrangements. Materials, drying, access and authorised work can affect the sequence. A preferred date is not a confirmed attendance or completion promise, and a cosmetic repair does not establish that the property is free of concealed defects.

Separate essential work, useful functional repairs and optional presentation work in the written proposal. That makes it possible to approve a coherent scope if the whole list cannot be completed together. Keep unfinished items visible in the handover record. For a sale or rental property, the appropriate professional assessment and disclosure decisions remain separate from the handyman repair record.

Home Safety Maintenance: Identify Immediate Hazards

Start with anything that could harm a person or rapidly damage the property. Examples may include a gas smell, sparking, active water near electricity, a ceiling that appears unstable, a broken external lock or significant storm damage. Keep people clear, isolate utilities only if you know how to do so safely, and contact the appropriate emergency service, utility or qualified trade.

Contact the Ellis Perth office when it is safe to arrange assessment and repairs. Explain any remaining hazard and confirm the visit details.

House Repairs: Active Damage Before Cosmetic Work

After immediate safety, address problems that are allowing damage to spread: ongoing leaks, failed weather seals, blocked drainage, pest entry points or openings that expose the home to rain. The visible symptom may have more than one cause, so avoid covering it cosmetically before the source is understood.

Next, consider essential daily function and security. A failed toilet in a one-bathroom home has a different priority from a stiff cupboard hinge. Heating, cooling and hot-water priorities also depend on weather and household needs. Tell Ellis if a household member needs priority assistance or if stairs, locked gates or occupied rooms affect access. Only share personal information needed to arrange the visit.

Combine Small Repair Jobs into a Home Maintenance List

Cosmetic patches, adjustments and minor installations can often be planned together when they suit the same skill set and access. Create a room-by-room list and mark each item as urgent, soon or planned. List a door that will not close, loose shelf or unstable handrail by its functional concern; a wall mark before sale photography is a separate presentation task. Record the location, preferred outcome and person approving each item. Keep electrical, plumbing and structural work in their appropriate scopes, and do not delay investigation of moisture or structural symptoms.

Contact Ellis Services Group with your Perth suburb and priority repair list. Our team arranges an on-site assessment, identifies the cause and confirms the repair plan, quote and appointment. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope.

Review the list after each visit. Close completed items, retain evidence for work that may recur and move newly identified tasks into the right priority band. If a temporary measure was used, record its limits and the required follow-up rather than allowing it to become an undocumented permanent repair.

Home Repair Priority Questions

Is an online request suitable for an emergency?

Do not assume so. Use the appropriate emergency service or utility for immediate danger, then arrange repairs when the site is safe.

Should I patch water damage straight away?

First identify and stop the moisture source. A cosmetic patch can hide continuing damage.

Can I bundle small jobs?

Often, if the tasks suit the same capability and access. List them separately; Ellis assesses the work on site and confirms the combined scope, qualified service arrangements and written quote.

Pre-sale Repair Checklists & Cosmetic House Repairs

Which minor repairs should I quote before selling a Perth home?

Start with faults affecting use, such as doors that will not close, loose fittings and damaged trim, then discuss the required finish. Ellis can assess the repair list before photography or an open home. Underlying defects should be identified, not concealed by cosmetic work.

Can you assess doors, skirting and wall marks before real-estate photography?

Yes. Explain the deadline and which items you want retained, repaired or refinished. We inspect the faults and agree the scope and appointment with you. Cosmetic preparation is not a promise of a sale price, faster sale or a defect-free inspection.

Which faults should not wait for a handyman appointment?

Immediate danger involving fire, electricity, gas, collapse or rapidly spreading water needs the appropriate emergency response. Keep clear and describe the hazard from safety. A routine repair enquiry does not replace emergency assistance.

Does an emergency handyman replace an emergency electrician or plumber?

No. The hazard determines which response and qualifications are needed. Use emergency services for immediate danger. Ellis confirms the appropriate own-team scope and appointment directly; an urgent general enquiry is not a specialist booking.

Related repair decisions

Requested deadlines and immediate hazards

For same-day, weekend or urgent repair requests, describe the fault, suburb, access and preferred time. Ellis confirms the accepted appointment directly; a requested day or a website enquiry does not reserve a slot.

A deadline this week, Saturday or before an inspection needs direct confirmation of suitable personnel and parts. A half-day request or urgent label does not establish job duration, a guaranteed arrival or a completed repair by that deadline.

A stuck room door before guests arrive and repairs requested before tomorrow’s rental inspection can have different urgency. Mention whether anyone is trapped, an exit is obstructed or the item is unstable so the correct response is identified.

What to take from this guide

An open-home deadline does not change the difference between active damage, functional faults and cosmetic work. Start with consequences and safety, then resolve the causes that could undo later repairs. Keep presentation work and any unfinished concerns visible in the agreed list. The FAQ questions explain priority choices and pre-sale scope, while the handyman service describes compatible small repairs. Arrange assessment early, confirm what has been authorised and avoid treating a fresh finish as evidence that an underlying defect has gone.
