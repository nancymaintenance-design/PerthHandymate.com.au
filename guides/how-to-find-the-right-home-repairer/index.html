A List of Small Home Repairs: Choosing a Handyman

Updated 2026-10-14

You have loose hinges, a shelf to hang and a damaged wall, and would like to deal with the list in one visit. The difficulty is deciding whether these are compatible small repair jobs or whether one item has a different cause, approval requirement or specialist boundary. A broad label such as general repairs does not settle that question.

What jobs does a handyman usually do, and when should you use a licensed trade instead? This guide shows how to describe your mixed list, compare the proposed work and confirm who will attend. It also explains why grouping jobs can help planning without guaranteeing a lower total or a particular appointment.

Choose against a real list, not a job title

Give each item its own location and observed fault. The kitchen cupboard hinge pulling away from its panel is different from a door that needs alignment; a shelf to be fitted is different from one that has already pulled out of the wall. A repairer needs those distinctions to decide what can be prepared together and what needs assessment before parts are selected.

Ask which items the proposed visit covers and which remain uncertain. If a wall patch sits below a recurring leak, explain that relationship before accepting a cosmetic scope. If shelving needs secure support, identify the item and intended use rather than selecting a fixing yourself. Clear descriptions let the attending team assess the substrate and the appropriate work.

Compare the response as well as the price

A useful response identifies the proposed assessment, access arrangements and how the written repair scope will be confirmed. Look for a clear answer about supplied parts, finishing and approval of additional work. An attractive total is hard to compare if one proposal includes paint making-good and another stops at the repaired board.

Keep a preferred date separate from a confirmed booking. For a rented or managed property, identify the person who can approve changes before arranging entry. Ellis confirms the scope and attending arrangements directly; relevant credentials can be checked for the actual task. The aim is a shared understanding of what the visit will decide and what repairs have been authorised.

Describe your small home repair jobs

You do not need to diagnose a fault before asking for help. Describe what you can see, hear or smell, where it happens and when it began. “The bedroom door scrapes the floor after rain” is more useful than guessing the repair method.

Mention whether the issue is worsening, affecting another room or creating a safety concern. Include the property type, access restrictions and previous related work if known. Photos and approximate measurements are optional; Ellis checks the affected area on site.

Handyman or Licensed Trade: Match the Repair Scope

Cupboard hinges, flat-pack assembly and picture hanging are examples of small handyman repair tasks. A new power point, persistent shower leak or unstable roof component needs its own assessment and work scope.

Ask who will complete each task and what credentials apply. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. The licensed-work guide explains how to check task-specific credentials. For a mixed list, identify work that depends on another repair being completed first.

Hiring a handyman: scope, quote & booking

Compare the scope, materials, exclusions, access assumptions and pricing basis as well as the total. Ask whether a figure is an estimate, assessment charge or written repair quote. Confirm any attendance or inspection charges before agreeing the visit.

Ellis confirms the attending team, assessment purpose and access arrangements directly. After checking the property, we confirm the repair plan and written quote. Keep the accepted scope and identify who can approve a variation if the findings change; a request or preferred date does not itself confirm a booking.

Choosing the Right Home Repairer: Common Questions

Do I need to know which trade I need?

No. Describe the symptoms and location clearly. Ellis can review the request, but the suitable service and Ellis Perth team still need to be confirmed.

Should I choose only on price?

Compare like-for-like scope, inclusions, exclusions, materials, credentials and communication as well as the total.

Who will attend an Ellis repair assessment?

Ellis confirms the attending team before the assessment. Everyday repairs are handled by our local handyman and maintenance team; regulated work is completed by our own appropriately licensed personnel for the agreed task.

Yes. Ellis assesses minor repairs, assembly and fittings as a single job list. Include the items that no longer work, your Perth suburb and any deadline. We confirm the suitable work, access and quote directly with you.

Can you help with a sticking door before guests arrive?

Call Ellis and explain which door is affected and when you need it working. We check the hinges, frame and clearance rather than assume a new door is needed. What work suits an ordinary handyman visit?

Defined minor repairs, assembly and suitable fittings can suit a handyman visit. Tell Ellis what is faulty and what you want changed. Regulated and safety-critical work requires a separate assessment and the appropriately qualified Ellis personnel.

When should I request a licensed trade rather than a small repair?

Tell Ellis if the fault involves fixed wiring, water services, gas, structural movement or safety equipment. These require their own assessment and appropriate qualifications. Our own licensed personnel handle regulated work within the agreed scope.

Choosing the right help for small repairs

Ordinary household repairs include loose fittings, furniture assembly, suitable wall mounting and minor patching. Describe the faulty item and desired result so Ellis can distinguish small repairs from a larger building or specialist task.

When comparing repairers, check the business identity, the proposed repair scope and who will perform any regulated work. A clear written quote is more useful than a broad claim that one person can fix everything.

A unit with a list of hinges, shelves and wall marks needs each fault recorded separately. Ellis checks the property, materials, permission and access before agreeing which jobs can be grouped.

What to take from this guide

Choosing a handyman starts with identifying the small repair jobs and the dependencies between them. A job title does not prove authority for every task, and bundling a list does not remove a leak, access problem or separate trade requirement. Compare the same scope, materials, finishing and approval process before accepting the work. Use the FAQ questions to clarify mixed-job planning, then review the handyman service for the type of repair you need and arrange assessment with your location and list.
