How to Find the Right Repairer for a Home Maintenance Job

A practical way to define the job, identify the right skill set and confirm who will attend before work begins.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Perth repair guide · Updated 2026-10-10

Scope note

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

Start with the problem, not a trade label

You do not need to diagnose a fault before asking for help. Describe what you can see, hear or smell, where it happens and when it began. “The bedroom door scrapes the floor after rain” is more useful than guessing the repair method.

Mention whether the issue is worsening, affecting another room or creating a safety concern. Include the property type, access restrictions and previous related work if known. Photos and approximate measurements are optional; Ellis checks the affected area on site.

Match the scope to the right capability

Cupboard hinges, flat-pack assembly and picture hanging are examples of small handyman repair tasks. A new power point, persistent shower leak or unstable roof component needs its own assessment and work scope.

Ask who will complete each task and what credentials apply. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. The licensed-work guide explains how to check task-specific credentials. For a mixed list, identify work that depends on another repair being completed first.

Confirm fit before accepting a booking

Compare the scope, materials, exclusions, access assumptions and pricing basis as well as the total. Ask whether a figure is an estimate, assessment charge or written repair quote. Confirm any attendance or inspection charges before agreeing the visit.

Ellis confirms the attending team, assessment purpose and access arrangements directly. After checking the property, we confirm the repair plan and written quote. Keep the accepted scope and identify who can approve a variation if the findings change; a request or preferred date does not itself confirm a booking.

Choosing the Right Home Repairer: Common Questions

Do I need to know which trade I need?

No. Describe the symptoms and location clearly. Ellis can review the request, but the suitable service and Ellis Perth team still need to be confirmed.

Should I choose only on price?

Compare like-for-like scope, inclusions, exclusions, materials, credentials and communication as well as the total.

Who will attend an Ellis repair assessment?

Ellis confirms the attending team before the assessment. Everyday repairs are handled by our local handyman and maintenance team; regulated work is completed by our own appropriately licensed personnel for the agreed task.

Local Handyman Services & Small Repair Jobs

Can a handyman in Perth take on small home repairs?

Yes. Ellis assesses minor repairs, assembly and fittings as a single job list. Include the items that no longer work, your Perth suburb and any deadline. We confirm the suitable work, access and quote directly with you.

Can you help with a sticking door before guests arrive?

Call Ellis and explain which door is affected and when you need it working. We check the hinges, frame and clearance rather than assume a new door is needed. Urgent attendance can be arranged from 30 minutes, subject to location, safe access and circumstances outside our control; confirm the visit time with our team.
