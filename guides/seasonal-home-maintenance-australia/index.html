A Seasonal Home Maintenance Plan for Australian Properties

Plan practical checks around local weather, property conditions and the skills required for each task.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Perth repair guide · Updated 2026-10-10

Scope note

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

Plan for your local climate

Australia does not have one maintenance calendar. Wet seasons, bushfire seasons, coastal exposure, frost, heat and storm patterns vary widely. Use local authority guidance and the property’s history to set timing. A useful plan combines regular visual checks with inspections by qualified people where access, safety or regulated systems are involved.

Before a locally wet or stormy period, look from safe ground for loose exterior elements, damaged seals, overflowing drainage signs and vegetation affecting access. Clear only what you can reach safely. Roofs, high gutters, power lines and unstable trees require appropriate expertise and equipment.

Check comfort, moisture and outdoor wear

Ahead of hotter or colder months, check doors and windows for obvious gaps, inspect accessible flyscreens, and note ventilation or condensation problems. Heating, cooling, electrical and gas equipment should be maintained according to manufacturer instructions and by appropriately authorised people where required. Never dismantle equipment for a seasonal check.

Outdoor timber, paint, paving, gates and fences can show early wear before failure. Record lifting surfaces, loose fixings, peeling coatings or drainage changes. In gardens, keep access paths visible and identify irrigation leaks or trip hazards. A cleaned gutter that still overflows needs the drainage concern assessed; oiling a deck does not address damaged boards or supports. A dropping gate or leaning fence also needs its supports considered. Avoid pressure cleaning fragile or unidentified older materials. Tree work, hazardous materials and structural damage require their own assessment rather than an ordinary garden tidy-up.

Turn observations into a workable list

Keep a dated list, with optional safe photos, and divide it into safety, water or weather exposure, essential function and planned presentation work. Note previous repair dates and known materials. This record helps explain whether an issue is new, recurring or stable and makes it easier to group compatible tasks.

Set reminders around local conditions rather than fixed national dates. After severe weather, note changes from safe ground and update the list when materials or access change.

Seasonal Home Maintenance Plan Questions

Is there one maintenance calendar for all Australia?

No. Adjust timing for local weather, exposure, authority guidance and the property’s history.

Should I inspect my roof myself?

Use safe ground-level observations. Work at height or on a damaged roof should be left to appropriately equipped Ellis Perth team members.

Torn Flyscreen Mesh & Flyscreen Remeshing

Can I replace just the mesh in my flyscreen frame?

Yes, where the frame and corners are sound, replacing mesh may be appropriate without replacing the frame. Ellis checks the mesh, frame, spline and fit before confirming the work. An ordinary flyscreen does not provide the protection of a security screen.

Can you quote for cat-damaged flyscreens before a rental inspection?

Yes. Count the damaged screens and tell us whether the frame is bent or only the mesh is torn. For a rental, confirm who can approve the work and provide access. We assess replacement mesh and fitting; completion timing is confirmed with you, not assumed from the inspection date.

Damaged Timber Skirting, Architraves & Local Timber Repairs

Can one damaged section of skirting board be replaced?

A damaged section can sometimes be replaced while retaining the rest, depending on the profile, material and available matching timber. Ellis checks the join, surrounding condition and finish. Mention moisture or soft timber so the cause is considered before a replacement is fitted.

Can you repair skirting gaps left after new flooring?

Yes. Tell us where gaps remain and whether the skirting was removed or damaged during flooring work. We assess the existing profile, floor level and required finish before agreeing repair or replacement. Painting and any additional flooring work should be identified in the quote.

Sagging Clothesline Wire & Folding Clothesline Repairs

Can a broken clothesline be repaired rather than replaced?

Repair may be appropriate if the frame, supports and mechanism can be retained. Ellis checks the line, brackets and moving parts before quoting restringing, hardware repair or replacement. Tell us whether the line sags, snaps or the unit no longer winds or folds.

Can you assess a wall-mounted clothesline coming loose from brickwork?

Yes. Stop using an unstable clothesline and explain which brackets have moved. We inspect the wall, anchors, frame and product requirements before agreeing repairs. Retensioning the line alone does not address a failing wall fixing.

Loose Fence Palings, Damaged Panels & Sagging Gates

Can one damaged fence panel be replaced in Perth?

A damaged section may be repairable if the remaining posts and rails provide suitable support. Ellis assesses the fence material, damage and access before quoting local replacement or a broader repair. Confirm approval arrangements for a shared boundary before work proceeds.

Can you repair a side gate that will not latch after rain?

Yes. We check the gate, hinges, latch and supporting posts to identify what has moved. Adjusting the latch alone may not correct a dropping frame or post. Pool gates and powered gates have separate safety requirements and are not treated as ordinary side gates.

Loose Decking Boards & Deck Timber Maintenance

Can a few rotten deck boards be replaced without rebuilding the deck?

A few boards may be replaceable where the supporting structure is sound and suitable matching timber is available. Ellis inspects boards, fixings and supports before agreeing repairs. Movement, sagging or extensive rot calls for a broader assessment rather than surface maintenance alone.

Can you assess deck screws lifting in an outdoor entertaining area?

Yes. Avoid the affected area if it is unstable and tell us where boards or fixings have moved. We check the timber and support as well as the screws. Oiling is agreed only after any required repair and preparation are identified.

Who can assess minor repairs to a Perth pergola?

Ellis can assess local pergola repairs, separating trim and lining work from damaged supports or structural movement. Describe loose timber, sagging components or recurring water damage. The assessment establishes the material and suitable work before any repair is approved.

Can you assess damaged eaves sheets on an older house?

Yes. Tell us the building age and visible damage without disturbing the sheets. The material needs identification before drilling, sanding or removal, because older linings may contain asbestos. We confirm the safe assessment and work required rather than assume a simple patch.

Can a handyman clean gutters on a single storey house in Perth?

Ellis assesses gutter cleaning on single-storey homes, checking safe access and the gutter condition before confirming the work. Tell us about leaf build-up, overflow and affected downpipes. Cleaning and repairing damaged drainage components are distinct items in the quote.

Why is the gutter above my front door still overflowing after cleaning?

A blockage elsewhere, gutter fall, outlet size or damaged components may be involved. Tell Ellis where overflow occurs and what was cleaned. We inspect the drainage path and identify the repair required rather than assume more leaf removal will solve it.
