How Property Managers Can Coordinate Maintenance Requests

Ellis Services Group receives your Perth maintenance request, assesses the work, confirms the repair scope and arranges the visit. A clear property record keeps owner approval, tenant access and completion evidence together.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Vacate Repair Lists, Strata Repairs & Office Access

Keep wall holes, torn screens, cabinet faults and loose fittings as separate items in the approved repair list. Name the contact who can approve cost and confirm tenant or building access. For strata, distinguish lot work from common property; for offices and shops, include trading hours, noise limits and mounting locations. Agree any completion-photo requirements before work. A completion record documents agreed repairs, not a bond decision, statutory inspection or guarantee that concealed defects are absent.

On-site assessment

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

Convert the report into a usable brief

Record the property address, affected room or exterior area, what the occupant has noticed, when it began and any continuing damage. Include previous related work where available. Ask only for photos the occupant can take from a stable, accessible position; they do not need to remove fittings, dismantle an item or investigate a hazard.

Group a mixed job list by location and priority. Ellis reviews the reported condition and explains the work and access needed. Keep diagnosis separate from the original report so the approved scope follows the site findings.

Keep assessment authorisation separate from repair authorisation

Assessment authorisation should identify the visit purpose, access, any agreed inspection fee and what findings are required. It does not automatically approve materials or repair work. Repair authorisation should identify the accepted scope, price or pricing basis, spending limit and person empowered to approve a change.

Add the owner's approval limit and escalation contact without sharing unrelated financial or tenant information. Where owner approval is needed, record it before confirming the repair. Our repair quote preparation guide helps you assemble the scope details.

Coordinate tenant access and changes

Confirm who may authorise entry, the access window, keys or concierge arrangements, pets, alarms, parking and relevant site rules. Give the occupant the Ellis team member's identity when confirmed and one contact path for changes. A preferred window becomes an appointment once Ellis confirms it.

If the tenant reschedules, access is unavailable or the property manager's contact changes, update the job record and confirm a revised appointment with Ellis. Keep entry arrangements consistent with the property's tenancy requirements; a maintenance request is not permission to enter at any time.

Completion photos should focus on the repair, avoiding personal belongings wherever practical. Share only the job and access information needed for the work.

Confirm hidden damage and additional work

If inspection or opening an agreed work area reveals hidden damage, Ellis records what has changed and explains the required repair. A written variation should state the additional work, materials, cost basis and effect on scheduling. The property manager secures approval from the authorised decision-maker before additional work proceeds.

Where a protective response is needed, confirm exactly what action is authorised. Temporary measures and the full repair remain separate entries: record the protection provided, remaining restriction and the work still required. Do not mark the entire request complete because one area has been made safe.

Use one handover record through to completion

Keep the tenant report, inspection findings, accepted quote, owner approval, access confirmation and variations together. Close each task against the approved scope rather than treating a mixed list as one undifferentiated job.

  • Property address, job reference, requested task and authorising contact.
  • Approved repair scope, price basis, purchase-order reference and billing recipient.
  • Work completed, materials or components addressed and relevant completion photos.
  • Written variations, owner approvals and any items excluded or not yet completed.
  • Temporary measures, restrictions, monitoring and follow-up owner or occupant action.
  • Completion date, recipient of the handover and invoice checked against the approved work.

Tell the occupant and owner what was completed and what remains. Store the record securely so the next request starts with useful job history.

Property Maintenance Coordination: Common Questions

What should a property manager include first?

Include the property address, observed problem, priority, safe photos, access contact, owner approval path and relevant job history.

Does assessment authorisation also authorise repairs?

No. Assessment authorisation covers the agreed inspection and any stated fee. Repair authorisation confirms the written work scope, price basis and spending limit before repairs begin.

What happens if Ellis finds hidden damage?

We record the finding and explain its effect on the repair scope. The property manager obtains the required owner approval for a written variation before additional work proceeds.

How are temporary measures recorded?

Record the measure taken, the area it protects, any remaining restrictions and the follow-up repair still required. A temporary measure does not close the original repair request.

Can one request cover a whole portfolio?

Yes. Use a separate record for each address, with its own tasks, access, approval limit and completion status. Agree portfolio arrangements with Ellis before booking.

Who receives the invoice and completion record?

Confirm the billing entity, purchase-order or job reference, approved scope, variations and recipient before booking. Match the invoice and handover record to those approvals.

Rental Maintenance & Vacate Repair Lists

Can a Perth handyman coordinate access with tenants?

Yes. Provide the authorised property contact and agreed access arrangements when booking. Ellis confirms the repair scope and quote with the person who can approve the work. Changes or additional repairs need approval; permission to enter is not approval for every task.

Can you quote for wall holes, torn flyscreens and a damaged cupboard before moving out?

Yes. List the affected items and confirm which repairs the landlord or property manager has authorised. We assess patching, screens and cabinet hardware as defined tasks and agree timing directly with you. Repair work does not determine whether your bond will be returned.

Strata Minor Repairs, Office Repairs & Shop Fittings

Can a handyman carry out approved strata maintenance in Perth?

Ellis can assess approved strata maintenance after the authorised contact confirms the work and access. Specify whether the item is within a lot or on common property. Fire doors, electrical systems and other specialist components require the appropriate assessment, not a general repair assumption.

Can you assemble office desks and hang whiteboards outside trading hours?

Tell Ellis the products, quantities, mounting locations and preferred access window. We check the fixing surfaces and confirm the booking directly. Include parking, building access and the person who can approve work; after-hours access does not remove noise or building restrictions.
