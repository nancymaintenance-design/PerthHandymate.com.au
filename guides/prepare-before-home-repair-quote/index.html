What to Prepare Before Asking for a Home Repair Quote

Give the Ellis Perth team the details needed to understand the job and explain price, scope and inspection requirements.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Perth repair guide · Updated 2026-10-12

Scope note

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

Build a short job brief

List the affected item, location, current condition and outcome you want. Note quantities, recurring faults and previous work if known. Photos and approximate measurements are optional; a description is enough to begin arranging an assessment.

Include the suburb or postcode, property type, access contact, parking, stairs, gates and pets where relevant. Mark a preferred date as a preference until Ellis confirms it. If supplying materials, identify product models, quantities and any missing hardware without buying further parts before suitability is checked.

Handyman Cost: Hourly, Half-day & Full-day Bookings

Handyman cost in Perth depends on the agreed repair list and how the booking is priced. The existing Ellis handyman service price range provides the published hourly, half-day and full-day labour guide; it is not a final quote or a promise that every task fits one block. Ellis confirms the hours or booking block and written scope after on-site assessment.

Ask what the agreed hours cover: preparation, fitting, cleanup and any agreed material collection. Confirm the duration and any minimum or attendance charge rather than assuming that a half-day or full-day label has a fixed number of included hours. Extra time and newly found work need their price and approval agreed before proceeding.

What about materials and supplied parts?

Separate labour from materials, equipment, removal and disposal, and confirm whether product supply is included. Customer-supplied parts still need compatibility checks; missing fixings or unsuitable products can change the scope. Regulated electrical, plumbing or other specialist work is itemised separately and completed by Ellis's own appropriately licensed personnel.

Will grouping repairs guarantee a lower price?

No discount is assumed. Bring a prioritised list so Ellis can assess which jobs suit the same visit. Compare quotes for the same hours, tasks, finish and material responsibility. For a managed property, use the approval and access checklist before confirming a booking.

Make comparisons possible

Compare the same repair list, materials and desired outcome. If you choose to share photos, use the same safe existing images and notes for each comparison. The optional-photo and scope guide explains useful framing and privacy.

Ask whether labour is charged by time or by the job and whether minimum, attendance or assessment charges apply. The written proposal should distinguish supplied items, labour, materials, equipment, preparation, removal, disposal, GST treatment where applicable, exclusions and separate trade work. See the handyman scope and price guide as a starting point; the quote follows assessment.

Bundling compatible repairs may reduce repeated setup, but does not guarantee a discount. Ask how newly discovered damage will change the work, price and timing. Approve the written variation before additional repairs proceed.

What Ellis needs for initial review

Share your location, repair list and the person arranging access. Ellis confirms the assessment appointment, checks the property and explains the repair plan and written quote. Confirm the visit purpose and any agreed assessment charge before booking.

Keep the initial brief, accepted scope and approval contact together. For a managed property, distinguish permission to assess from permission to repair. If a comparison assumes a different material or work scope, clarify it before comparing the totals.

Home Repair Quote Preparation: Common Questions

Can I get a final price from photos?

Photos are optional and do not establish a final repair price. Ellis checks the affected area, measurements and access on site, then confirms the repair plan and written quote.

What should a written scope include?

The task, location, quantities, materials, inclusions, exclusions, access assumptions and how variations will be approved.

Does Ellis provide instant online quotes?

Ellis confirms your assessment appointment directly. Send the service needed and Perth postcode so our team can arrange the visit, check the job and confirm the quote.

Itemised Handyman Quotes & Minimum-charge Questions

How much does a handyman cost in Perth?

The cost depends on the agreed task, materials, access and any separate trade work. Ellis provides a written quote after assessment. Ask us to confirm whether the work is priced by time or by the job and whether attendance, assessment or minimum charges apply before approval.

Can I request a labour-only quote using materials I supply?

Yes. Tell Ellis exactly what you will supply, including product models and quantities. We check whether the parts suit the job and identify any missing materials. The written scope should distinguish supplied items, labour, disposal and any additional charges before work starts.

View booking details

Is grouping small repairs always cheaper?

It may reduce repeated setup when the tasks and access suit each other. Materials, different trades or return stages can change the quote. Ellis confirms the charging basis and included work; no automatic bundle discount is assumed.

What should I include in my repair list?

List each faulty item by room, what happens, how many are affected and your preferred result. Include priorities, suburb, access and approval details plus supplied-product information. Photos and measurements are optional.

What should a handyman quote include?

Look for the agreed tasks, labour basis, materials, supply, access, removal, disposal and exclusions, plus the approval process for changes. Ask about any minimum or visit charge before booking. Ellis confirms the written quote after assessment.

How can I check the business and relevant qualifications?

Confirm the business identity and ask who will carry out each regulated task, with the applicable licence or registration details for that scope. A business name alone proves no trade qualification. Ellis identifies its own appropriate personnel during assessment.

Related repair decisions

Grouping jobs without losing the detail

Several compatible small repairs can be assessed together. A room-by-room list helps organise cupboard adjustments, wall patching, assembly and hanging work without treating different materials or methods as one identical job.

Ask for the included tasks, materials and charging basis in writing. Shared access and preparation may reduce repeated setup, but grouping jobs does not establish a discount, minimum charge or a promise that everything fits one visit.

For hinges, shelves and a wall patch in the same home, mark the priority and quantity of each item. A requested half-day is a preference to discuss; parts, drying stages and specialist work may affect the agreed booking.
