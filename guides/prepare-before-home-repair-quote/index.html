Build a short job brief

Write down the outcome you want, the current condition and the parts of the property affected. Add approximate measurements, the number of items, preferred materials if any and whether you want supply, installation, removal or disposal included. For a recurring fault, note when it occurs and what has already been tried.

Include your suburb and postcode, property type, parking or access restrictions, stairs, pets and whether someone must coordinate entry. Mention deadlines as preferences unless they are genuinely fixed. Avoid presenting a desired date as confirmed availability; Ellis Perth team needs to assess the scope and schedule first.

Make comparisons possible

Our Ellis Perth team reviews your brief and prepares a written proposal covering labour, materials, equipment, site preparation, disposal, GST treatment where applicable, exclusions and site assumptions. We assess the property and explain the pricing basis, repair scope and any agreed inspection or access work before you approve the repairs.

Price is only one part of fit. Look at the proposed method, relevant experience, communication, timing assumptions and any required licences. Ask how a variation will be raised and approved if the condition behind a wall, fixture or surface differs from what was visible. Do not authorise extra work through vague verbal agreement.

What Ellis needs for initial review

Contact Ellis Perth to arrange your repair assessment. Share the property location and job details; our team confirms the visit time, checks the work on site and provides a quote for the agreed scope.

Before proceeding, confirm who would attend, whether an inspection is needed, what is included and what remains unknown. If regulated work may be involved, confirm the credentials relevant to that work. Keeping the initial brief and the accepted scope together creates a useful record for the homeowner, property manager and Ellis Perth team.

A final pre-quote check is simple: confirm the correct address, contact person, access window, number of items, preferred outcome and any safety concern. Attach the same photo set and notes to every comparison. If one response assumes a different material or scope, ask for it to be revised before comparing totals.

Home Repair Quote Preparation: Common Questions

Can I get a final price from photos?

Send safe photos to Ellis to help arrange the site assessment. We check access, measurements and the affected area on site, identify the work required and confirm your quote.

What should a written scope include?

The task, location, quantities, materials, inclusions, exclusions, access assumptions and how variations will be approved.

Does Ellis provide instant online quotes?

Ellis confirms your assessment appointment directly. Send the service needed and Perth postcode so our team can arrange the visit, check the job and confirm the quote.

Flat-pack Furniture Assembly & Wardrobe Assembly

How much does flat pack furniture assembly cost in Perth?

The quote depends on the item type, quantity, assembly instructions, supplied parts and any anchoring required. Tell Ellis the product model and where it will be assembled. We separate furniture assembly from kitchen fitting, modifications and electrical work.

Can you assemble a bed and desk after I move house?

Yes. Tell us the number of items, product models and whether they are new kits or partly dismantled furniture. Keep instructions and supplied hardware together if available. We check room access, missing parts and assembly requirements before confirming the quote.
