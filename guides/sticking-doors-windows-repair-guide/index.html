Sticking Doors & Timber Windows: What Needs Repair?

A door or window that catches may need hardware adjustment, a local timber repair or attention to moisture and movement. The place it catches and when the problem occurs are more useful than guessing which part to replace.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Updated 2026-10-10

Before attempting repairs

This guide helps you describe a fault and plan an assessment. It does not diagnose your property or replace task-specific safety and trade requirements.

Sticking internal doors and hinge adjustment

A door that rubs after new carpet needs a different assessment from one that has dropped on loose hinges. Record where contact occurs, whether the latch lines up and what recently changed. The frame, hinges, door construction and floor clearance all affect the proposed repair. Do not assume trimming is appropriate for every door. Security and fire-rated components have specific requirements that must be checked before modification.

Timber windows swelling after wet weather

If a window becomes difficult to close after rain, describe the timing and any visible damp, cracked finish or soft timber. Moisture entry may affect the sill, frame or surrounding materials. Planing or repainting without assessing the cause may leave the same fault recurring. Hardware, sash movement and glazing can involve different repair scopes. Do not force a swollen or unstable window simply to make it shut.

Sliding doors hard to open: rollers, track and alignment

Dragging can come from worn rollers, track damage, debris or misalignment. Identify whether the affected panel is glass or an insect screen and whether it has left its track. Roller replacement does not necessarily require replacing the whole door, but compatible hardware and a serviceable track matter. Heavy glass and mirrored panels require appropriate handling. Keep clear of unstable panels rather than lifting or forcing them yourself.

Repair, replacement and access details

Tell Ellis the door or window type, the symptoms, how long the fault has been present and what you want retained. Existing photos are useful but not required. Include apartment lifts, parking, building access and the person authorised to approve work. A written quote should distinguish adjustments, replacement hardware, timber work, glazing and finishing. Repeated moisture or movement needs to be considered before choosing a cosmetic repair.

Common Repair Questions

Why does a timber door stick in wet weather?

Moisture can affect timber and its finish, but hinge or frame problems may also be involved. Note when it sticks and where it rubs. Assessment should consider the cause before trimming or repainting.

Does a damaged sliding-door track always need replacement?

Not always. The extent of damage, panel alignment and available compatible components determine whether local repair or replacement is appropriate. Roller work alone may not correct a damaged track.

Should I call a handyman, carpenter or glazier for a window repair?

Describe the fault rather than choosing a trade from the window name alone. Timber, hardware and glass can need different capabilities. Ellis confirms the appropriate work after reviewing the issue.

Sticking Internal Doors & Door Hinge Adjustment

Who can fix a door that will not close in Perth?

Ellis can inspect a door that will not close and assess the hinges, latch, frame and clearance. Tell us whether it rubs, drops or misses the latch. A local adjustment and installing a new door are different scopes; the inspection determines which is appropriate.

Can you assess a bedroom door rubbing after new carpet?

Yes. Mention the flooring change and show where the door catches. We check the clearance, hinges and door construction before proposing an adjustment. Fire-rated and security doors need their specific requirements checked; they are not treated as ordinary internal doors.

Sliding Doors Hard to Open & Worn Rollers

Can sliding door rollers be replaced without replacing the door?

Often the rollers can be assessed separately from the door, but the track, frame and matching hardware also matter. Ellis checks what has worn and whether the panel is suitable to retain. Heavy glass panels require an appropriate handling method, not force to release a jammed door.

My apartment balcony door is jammed. What should I tell Ellis?

Tell us whether the glass panel or insect screen is stuck, whether it can close securely and if it has left the track. Include apartment access and building approval requirements. Keep clear of an unstable panel and arrange an assessment before trying to lift it.

Sticking Timber Windows & Damaged Window Sills

Who can repair an old timber window in Perth?

Ellis assesses old timber windows, including the frame, sill, moving parts and hardware. Explain whether the window sticks, leaks or no longer closes securely. Local timber and hardware repairs are considered alongside any glazing or more extensive replacement work required.

My timber window swells after rain. Can you assess the cause?

Yes. Describe when it sticks, where water appears and whether the sill feels soft. The assessment considers moisture entry as well as the moving parts. Repeatedly trimming or repainting the window without addressing moisture may not resolve the recurring fault.

Pet Flaps in Timber Doors & Ordinary Flyscreens

Can a pet door be fitted into an existing timber door?

Ellis assesses the door material, panel construction and product before fitting a pet door. Tell us the pet size and proposed position. Glass, security screens and fire-rated doors require their specific requirements to be checked, rather than treated as a standard timber cut-out.

Can you replace a cat flap in an ordinary flyscreen?

Yes, subject to the screen condition and a suitable product. We check the opening, frame and mesh support before confirming fitting. Explain if the screen is also a security door so its safety and security requirements can be assessed separately.

Can you install a pet door in glass or a security screen?

Ellis checks the panel type and safety or security requirements before agreeing the installation. Some glass installations need a purpose-made replacement pane. We confirm the appropriate installation method, components and price before work begins.

Can a pet door be cut into an existing toughened pane?

No ordinary on-site cut-out should be planned. Holes are prepared before toughening, as explained in the manufacturer’s toughened-glass guidance. Ellis’s glazing assessment identifies a suitable replacement-pane and pet-door scope where needed; do not drill or cut the existing pane.

Could a pet door affect a security door?

Yes. The alteration can affect the complete security product. Ellis checks the product and applicable work requirements before agreeing any change. Do not assume fitting a pet flap preserves the door’s security performance.
