Damaged Cabinet under a Leaking Sink: Handyman or Licensed Trade in Perth?

Updated 2026-10-14

The cabinet below a sink is damaged and the sink is leaking. Repairing the cabinet can be one part of the work, but it does not explain or stop the water source. A similar split occurs when a TV bracket installation also needs a new power point: the mounting task and electrical change need distinct scopes.

Does a handyman business name prove plumbing or electrical licensing? No: the work itself determines which credentials need checking. This guide explains how to separate dependent repairs, verify the relevant practitioner details and agree the order of work without treating a broad maintenance description as permission for every task.

Scope note

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

Write two connected tasks instead of one vague repair

For the sink example, record the water symptom and the cabinet damage separately, then explain that they occur in the same location. The cause of the leak needs assessment before the damaged material is repaired or covered. A cabinet that looks dry during the visit may still have a history that matters. Share when damp appears and any earlier plumbing or cabinet work without opening fittings to investigate.

Ask how the proposed work handles this dependency: who assesses the water source, what must be confirmed before cabinet work and who approves additional damage discovered later. For TV mounting, state whether the existing power point will be used or a new electrical change is requested. The bracket, wall support and electrical work should each be clear in the agreed scope.

Check the person and the task before work starts

Use the official regulator resources linked below to check the relevant details for the actual work. A business name, directory category or registration number is not an answer to a task-specific licensing question. Ask the attending business to identify the practitioner and explain the relevant work category, then resolve any mismatch before the regulated work begins.

For a mixed visit, the written record should show what has been approved and which items remain subject to specialist assessment. Keep certificates or other job documents supplied for the agreed work with that record. Do not disconnect services, expose wiring or disturb suspect material to make the visit easier. If there is an immediate hazard, use the appropriate emergency or specialist response rather than waiting for ordinary maintenance.

The task matters more than the label

The exact work determines the required capability. Separate a TV bracket from a new power point, a cupboard repair from a leaking sink, and surface resealing from concealed wet-area repairs. Fire doors, security components, structural repairs and suspected asbestos also need their specific requirements checked.

For a grab rail, the product, position and supporting wall matter; a towel rail is not a substitute. Ordinary maintenance is not automatically a funded home modification. Confirm funding, plan approval and any required installation documents separately before agreeing work.

Keep people clear of exposed wiring, gas smells, active water near electrical fittings, structural movement or suspected hazardous material. Seek the appropriate emergency service, utility or authorised advice for immediate danger. Do not investigate physically or ask anyone to work beyond their authorised scope.

Check the correct credential for the work

Ask for the attending practitioner or business name and the credential relevant to the exact task. Use the WA Building and Energy licence and registration search for relevant electrical, plumbing, gas and building lookups. Check the holder, current status and licence class rather than relying on a screenshot alone. Ask the regulator if a category or scope is unclear.

An ABN identifies a business; it does not replace a trade licence. Ellis discusses the credentials and project documents relevant to the agreed work before it begins.

How Ellis Delivers Licensed Work

Contact Ellis Perth with the property location and observed problem. Photos and measurements are optional. We arrange the on-site assessment and confirm who will attend, the visit purpose and access.

Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. Discuss electrical work and plumbing repairs as separate tasks from ordinary handyman work, even when they share one request.

Before repairs start, confirm the written scope, quote and relevant credentials or documents. If the findings introduce regulated work or change the agreed repair, pause the affected task and approve the revised scope and price in writing.

Licensed Trade Requirements: Common Questions

Is every handyman task licensed?

No. Requirements depend on the task, value, location and other factors. Check the relevant state or territory regulator.

Does an ABN prove a trade licence?

No. Business registration and trade licensing are different checks.

How do I confirm the right licence for work by Ellis?

Ask Ellis to identify the attending personnel and credentials for the agreed task. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work. Check the relevant credential through the official regulator before work starts.

TV Bracket Fitting & Separate Electrical Work

Can a handyman replace a power point in Perth?

A power point replacement is electrical work, separate from TV bracket fitting. Tell Ellis what needs changing so we can confirm the electrical work, applicable licensing requirements and quote before work starts.

Does TV bracket fitting include a new power point?

No. Bracket fitting and installing or changing a power point are distinct scopes. Tell Ellis both requirements so the electrical work is assessed and quoted appropriately. Do not alter fixed wiring or ask for it to be treated as a simple wall-mounting task.

What should I confirm before requesting an NDIS-funded home repair?

Confirm funding eligibility, invoicing, registration requirements and approval with your plan contact before booking. Then share the approved repair list and any installation plan with Ellis. We confirm the work and access directly; funding approval is a separate step from the property assessment.

When do pergola repairs require structural advice?

Sagging, movement, damaged supports or connections and changes to roof loads need more than a trim repair. Keep clear of unstable areas. Ellis scopes the appropriate assessment before agreeing repair work.

Could older eaves lining contain asbestos?

Yes. A photo or the age of the house alone cannot rule it out. Leave unidentified lining undisturbed and arrange appropriate assessment before repair. See WorkSafe asbestos guidance; do not cut, drill, sand or pressure clean suspect material.

Which older materials should not be pressure cleaned?

Do not pressure clean suspected asbestos-containing material or disturb unidentified material before assessment. See WorkSafe asbestos guidance. Fragile coatings and weak joints also need their method checked; roof and asbestos cleaning are outside this patio-and-paving scope.

Is handyman maintenance the same as a funded home modification?

No. A funded modification depends on individual needs and arrangements; routine maintenance does not establish eligibility. See NDIS home-modification guidance. Confirm the funding and payment requirements separately with Ellis before booking; registration or reimbursement is not assumed.

Why are grab-rail placement and fixings assessed first?

The location must suit the intended use and appropriate plan; the wall must support the chosen product and fixings. Ellis checks both before installation. A visually solid wall finish is insufficient, and installation alone is not an accessibility assessment.

Can a bathroom towel rail serve as a support rail?

Do not rely on it for balance or transfer support. A suitable grab rail needs its own product, placement and structural fixing assessment. Tell Ellis the intended use and bring any adviser’s plan before installation is agreed.

Related repair decisions

Patio trim, supports and unidentified eaves lining

Suitable minor patio, pergola and eaves repairs start with the damaged component and material identified. Loose trim differs from a failing beam, post, roof connection or unidentified old lining.

Ellis assesses support, moisture history and material before agreeing local work. Structural movement and suspected hazardous material need the appropriate own-team assessment and controls before disturbance; a small visible area does not establish a small-risk repair.

Weathered trim under a patio and damaged eaves sheet on an older house need different checks. Keep clear of loose overhead material. Do not remove, sand or drill unidentified lining to prepare photos; existing safely taken photos are optional.

Repairs for older residents and support-rail enquiries

You can arrange suitable small household repairs for an older family member. Identify who approves the work, who will be present and any communication or access needs. A loose handrail needs its support assessed before anyone relies on it.

Grab rails need a suitable product, supporting structure and placement plan. Routine repairs do not establish funded home-modification eligibility, company NDIS registration or payment acceptance. Check individual arrangements separately; NDIS home-modification guidance distinguishes modification needs from ordinary maintenance.

For a loose front-step handrail or household fittings needing repair for an older resident, describe the risk and keep the unstable fitting out of use. Bring any adviser’s rail plan; location advice and installation are separate assessments, without an accessibility guarantee.
