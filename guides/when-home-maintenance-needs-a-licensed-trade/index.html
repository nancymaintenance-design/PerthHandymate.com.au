When Does Home Maintenance Need a Licensed Trade?

Understand why licensing depends on the task and location, and what to confirm before regulated work starts.

Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment in Perth, identify the cause and confirm the repair plan and quote.

Perth repair guide · Updated 2026-10-10

Scope note

This guide is for general reference only. Actual conditions vary. Contact Ellis Services Group to arrange an on-site assessment by the appropriate trade professional.

The task matters more than the label

The exact work determines the required capability. Separate a TV bracket from a new power point, a cupboard repair from a leaking sink, and surface resealing from concealed wet-area repairs. Fire doors, security components, structural repairs and suspected asbestos also need their specific requirements checked.

For a grab rail, the product, position and supporting wall matter; a towel rail is not a substitute. Ordinary maintenance is not automatically a funded home modification. Confirm funding, plan approval and any required installation documents separately before agreeing work.

Keep people clear of exposed wiring, gas smells, active water near electrical fittings, structural movement or suspected hazardous material. Seek the appropriate emergency service, utility or authorised advice for immediate danger. Do not investigate physically or ask anyone to work beyond their authorised scope.

Check the correct credential for the work

Ask for the attending practitioner or business name and the credential relevant to the exact task. Use the WA Building and Energy licence and registration search for relevant electrical, plumbing, gas and building lookups. Check the holder, current status and licence class rather than relying on a screenshot alone. Ask the regulator if a category or scope is unclear.

An ABN identifies a business; it does not replace a trade licence. Ellis discusses the credentials and project documents relevant to the agreed work before it begins.

How Ellis Delivers Licensed Work

Contact Ellis Perth with the property location and observed problem. Photos and measurements are optional. We arrange the on-site assessment and confirm who will attend, the visit purpose and access.

Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work within the agreed scope. Discuss electrical work and plumbing repairs as separate tasks from ordinary handyman work, even when they share one request.

Before repairs start, confirm the written scope, quote and relevant credentials or documents. If the findings introduce regulated work or change the agreed repair, pause the affected task and approve the revised scope and price in writing.

Licensed Trade Requirements: Common Questions

Is every handyman task licensed?

No. Requirements depend on the task, value, location and other factors. Check the relevant state or territory regulator.

Does an ABN prove a trade licence?

No. Business registration and trade licensing are different checks.

How do I confirm the right licence for work by Ellis?

Ask Ellis to identify the attending personnel and credentials for the agreed task. Our own appropriately licensed personnel complete regulated work. Check the relevant credential through the official regulator before work starts.

Home Safety Maintenance & Grab Rail Fixing Assessment

Can I arrange handyman repairs for an elderly parent in Perth?

Yes. A family member can arrange a household repair enquiry, nominate the person who approves work and discuss suitable access with Ellis. For grab rails, supply an existing placement plan if available. Funding eligibility and approval are separate from our assessment of the repair.

Can you assess a loose handrail at the front steps?

Yes. Explain where it moves and whether it is needed for support. Avoid relying on an unstable rail. We assess the rail, supporting material and fixing requirements before agreeing repairs; a towel rail or improvised fitting is not a suitable substitute.

TV Bracket Fitting & Separate Electrical Work

Can a handyman replace a power point in Perth?

A power point replacement is electrical work, separate from TV bracket fitting. Tell Ellis what needs changing so we can confirm the electrical work, applicable licensing requirements and quote before work starts.

Does TV bracket fitting include a new power point?

No. Bracket fitting and installing or changing a power point are distinct scopes. Tell Ellis both requirements so the electrical work is assessed and quoted appropriately. Do not alter fixed wiring or ask for it to be treated as a simple wall-mounting task.
