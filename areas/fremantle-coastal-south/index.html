Window, timber and exterior repairs around Fremantle

Assess window, timber and exterior repairs by condition

For a coastal home or established property, weathered timber and stiff window fittings may be visible, but the repair still depends on the material and surrounding condition. Tell Ellis whether the concern is a loose fitting, damaged finish, a gap or a part that no longer moves correctly. Mention earlier repairs if known. A broad coastal location alone does not establish why a particular component has failed.

For an apartment, identify whether the affected window, screen or exterior surface is within your own property or involves a shared area. Add the floor, lift or stair arrangements and the person handling building-management permission where relevant. Exterior work may require a different access plan from an internal hardware repair. Existing photos from a safe position can help explain the location without requiring you to reach outside or remove a damaged part.

Sticking windows, damaged screens and paint finishes

If exterior timber needs painting after repair, open exterior painting and preparation alongside the carpentry service below. Note whether you want to retain the existing colour and finish. Preparation and compatible coatings form part of the assessment; painting over a damaged surface is not assumed to solve the underlying issue.

For chipped or cracked masonry finishes, wall rendering and render repairs provides a separate service page. Tell Ellis whether the damage is localised, recurring or accompanied by visible damp. Texture, substrate and access affect what can be repaired and how the finish is discussed. A roof-edge overflow or leaking downpipe belongs under gutter cleaning and repairs, rather than being bundled into a wall-finish request without investigation.

Confirm shared boundaries and repair access

On an established property, explain which windows, trim or finishes you want to keep. Repairs, partial replacements and broader upgrades have different material and finishing requirements. Ellis checks the items on site and confirms suitable work in the written quote. Give the actual suburb or postcode, access contact and priority items so the assessment can address the property itself rather than rely on the regional description.
