Coordinate indoor and outdoor maintenance in the northern suburbs

Give each outdoor item its own location

A request covering gutters, a garden and a fence is easier to assess when each item has a clear location. Describe the affected side of the house, the fence section or the part of the garden needing attention. For a gutter concern, explain whether you noticed overflow, a leaking join or a loose section from ground level. Avoid climbing to confirm the cause; Ellis assesses safe access and the work required.

For properties along the northern coastal and residential growth corridor, the property layout matters more than a broad area label. Tell us how the side or rear garden is reached, whether a gate limits entry, and where materials or garden waste could be carried. If outdoor work is near occupied rooms or a frequently used path, identify those areas so the proposed sequence can be discussed at assessment.

Distinguish upkeep from a new outdoor project

A damaged fence section can be assessed as a repair enquiry. A different boundary layout or a new structure needs its own scope. Similarly, deck repairs and building should start with whether you want existing boards assessed or a new deck considered. Describe visible loose boards and the extent of the area without testing damaged timber under load.

For uneven paths or an outdoor paved area, open paving repairs and installation. Note where the surface is uneven and any drainage symptoms you have observed. A local paving repair depends on the base and surrounding levels, so the assessment establishes whether lifting and relaying is suitable. If the indoor list includes damaged mesh, flyscreen repairs provides details about door or window screens.

Prioritise the visit and the quote

Tell Ellis which problem affects daily use first and which jobs are routine upkeep. Garden clean-up, gutter work and timber repairs may involve different access and materials even when requested together. Include your actual suburb or postcode and the person who can approve the work. The team confirms the assessment arrangements and separates agreed repairs from optional improvements in the written quote.

Indoor repairs and outdoor maintenance

You can combine an indoor repair enquiry with damaged flyscreen mesh, a fence section or deck maintenance that needs assessment. Specify which areas need attention and how the team can access the outdoor space. We distinguish maintenance from new construction in the quote.
