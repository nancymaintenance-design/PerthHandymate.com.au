Organise grouped repairs before a move or sale

Turn a property walkthrough into a useful list

For homes across the eastern suburbs and toward the Swan corridor, start with the actual property layout and the items needing attention. Walk through accessible rooms and list the affected door, cupboard, trim or wall by location. Distinguish something that does not operate correctly from a surface you would like refreshed. That makes it easier to discuss essential repairs and presentation work without assuming every item needs replacement.

If the request is timed around a move, tell Ellis whether the property will be occupied, partly packed or empty at assessment. Boxes and furniture can affect access to hinges, cupboards and wall surfaces. You do not need to move heavy furniture to prepare an enquiry. Explain the access available and who will be present to identify the repair list and approve the quote.

Follow the item to the appropriate service

For a door adjustment or damaged frame, open door repairs and installation. Describe whether the door rubs, fails to latch or has visible material damage. For cupboard hinges, drawer operation or fitted storage changes, use cabinet repairs and joinery. Tell Ellis which existing surfaces and handles you want retained.

Wall marks and holes may require patching before the painting service below is useful. wall and ceiling patching explains that part of the scope. State whether the aim is a local repair ready for paint or a wider finished wall area. Where outside areas also need attention, garden clean-up can be listed separately with the location of the vegetation or waste and the access path for carrying it out.

Keep timing and finish expectations explicit

Share any move or sale-preparation target as a scheduling need, then let the assessment establish the work sequence and materials. A preferred date does not confirm attendance or completion. Safely taken photos and existing paint details are useful if available, but optional. Ellis confirms the repairs, finishing expectations and written quote with you; the agreed scope relates to the property work rather than a sale or inspection outcome.

Grouped repairs before moving or sale preparation

List door adjustments, cabinet hardware, damaged trim or fittings you want assessed before moving house or preparing a property for sale. Tell us what should be retained and the finish you want. We assess the repair work and confirm the quote and timing with you.

Explore Small handyman repairs, Carpentry and timber repairs, Painting and touch-ups. Open the service that matches your repair to see the work Ellis can assess and what affects the quote.

Existing photos are optional. You can describe the problem and ask our Perth team to assess it on site. We confirm access, the required work and a written quote with you.
