Separate supplied products from repairs to the property

A household move or furniture update can produce a mixed list of assembly, fixing and repair work. For a supplied item, provide the product name, whether the boxes are complete and where the finished item will be used. For an existing shelf or loose fitting, identify the wall or fixing surface and the visible problem. These are different assessment questions even when the items sit in the same room.

Across the southern metropolitan and coastal residential areas, include the actual suburb or postcode and access to each part of the property. Furniture may need to pass through a doorway or staircase; outdoor repairs may use a side gate instead. Tell Ellis about these access arrangements before agreeing the scope. Keep product instructions and hardware available if you have them, without unpacking heavy items or checking damaged fixings under load.
