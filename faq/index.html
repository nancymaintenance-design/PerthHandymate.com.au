Perth Handyman FAQs

Clear answers about booking a local handyman, pricing, service areas and when licensed work is required.

Hiring & matching

Hiring & matching

How do I book a handyman in Perth?

Tell the Ellis Perth office what needs fixing, your suburb or postcode and a plain-language description. Safe photos, approximate measurements and access notes help our local team discuss the job with you.

Do I need to choose the correct trade myself?

No. Describe what you observe. You can speak directly with our Perth office and local team, who will confirm the job scope and any licensed-work requirements before booking.

Who will handle my handyman job?

For everyday repairs and maintenance, you deal directly with the Ellis Perth local handyman and maintenance team. If the job requires a licensed specialist, we explain that requirement before you book.

Who will arrive for my booking?

Your booking confirmation names the Ellis Perth team member or explains any licensed specialist requirement, together with the agreed scope and arrival details.

How do I know the right person is handling my job?

Confirm the written scope, relevant experience, any required credentials, proposed timing and how questions or variations will be handled before work starts.

Does sending a request confirm a booking?

Not yet. The Ellis Perth office first confirms the job details, suburb or postcode and practical next step with you.

Quotes & scope

Quotes & scope

What details help with an initial assessment?

Include the desired outcome, current condition, location, quantities, approximate dimensions, materials if known, safe photos and access constraints.

How is my project price worked out?

Share the task, postcode, measurements and safe photos. Ellis reviews the scope and confirms whether an inspection is needed before a price is provided.

What should I check in a quote?

Check the scope, labour and materials, inclusions, exclusions, GST treatment where applicable, timing assumptions, payment terms and variation process.

What is the difference between a quote and an estimate?

The terms can have different implications. Ask Ellis Perth team to label the document clearly and explain which conditions could change the amount.

Can a photo replace an inspection?

Not always. Measurements, access, concealed conditions, safety concerns or regulated work may require an on-site assessment.

What happens if extra work is found?

Ask for the reason, revised scope, price and timing impact in writing before authorising a variation, unless an agreed safety action is required.

Licensed trades

Licensed trades

Which home jobs need a licence?

Requirements vary by task and state or territory. Electrical, plumbing, gas, structural and other work may be regulated; check the relevant official authority.

Does an ABN prove someone is licensed?

No. An ABN identifies a business for tax and registration purposes; it does not replace a task-specific trade licence check.

Will I know who is coming to my property?

Yes. Your booking confirmation identifies the Ellis Perth team member or explains any licensed specialist requirement. Ask us before booking if anything is unclear.

How should I check a licence?

Use the official register for the state or territory and confirm the name, number, status, class and scope match Ellis Perth team and work.

How do I check licences and insurance for my job?

Where licensed work is required, Ellis Perth explains the specialist requirement before booking. Check the licences and insurance that apply to the agreed scope before work starts.

What should I do if the job changes into regulated work?

Pause the affected work and confirm that the revised scope will be completed by an appropriately authorised Ellis Perth team, with required documentation.

Property managers

Property managers

Can property managers send requests for multiple properties?

Yes, but each property and scope should be recorded separately so serviceability, access, Ellis Perth team and approval details remain clear.

What access information should I provide?

Provide the authorised contact, permitted window, key or concierge process, parking, alarms, pets and relevant site rules.

Can Ellis contact the tenant directly?

Contact arrangements must be agreed for the specific request, with only the information needed for access and service delivery shared.

How are approval limits handled?

State the approval pathway and limit in the brief. Any variation should be documented and authorised by the nominated person before extra work proceeds.

What completion evidence can I request?

Agree on completion notes, relevant photos, invoices and any required compliance documents before the job starts.

How can property managers arrange multi-property service?

Share the properties, service types and approval process. Agree coverage, response expectations, invoicing and any service-level terms in writing.
